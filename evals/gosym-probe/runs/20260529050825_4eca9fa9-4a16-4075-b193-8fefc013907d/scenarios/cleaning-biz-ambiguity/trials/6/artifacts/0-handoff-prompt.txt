PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business name: Shine Bright Cleaning
- Service type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Generate leads (calls/contacts)

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, trustworthy, and spotlessly fresh. Should feel like a reliable neighbor you'd hand your house keys to — not a cold corporate service. Clean whites and airy layouts that reinforce the "clean" brand, with warm accents that feel approachable and personal.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business that needs full navigation structure with header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp whites and soft sky blues as the base, with a warm golden-yellow accent to convey sunshine and brightness. Avoid cold sterile blues — keep it warm and inviting.
- Typography: Friendly but professional — a rounded or soft sans-serif for headings that feels approachable, clean sans for body. Confident but never corporate.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a staggered two-column layout with photos and text alternating. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTAs. Use an asymmetric CTA with a photo element.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, airy photos of clean homes, sparkling surfaces, and natural light-filled rooms
- Style: Warm, natural lighting; real home interiors (not sterile stock); before/after feel
- Application: Full-bleed hero with overlay, alternating photo/text in services section, photo accent in CTA
- Logo: Clean, modern wordmark with a small sun or sparkle icon. Use the media-logo skill to generate the logo — do NOT substitute a text wordmark or emoji.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for each section (300ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y -4px + soft shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — motion should feel like a deep breath, not a sales pitch

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed photo of a beautifully clean bright living room, headline overlay with strong value prop ("Your Home, Spotlessly Clean"), phone number prominently displayed, subtle fade-in entrance animation
- Services: Staggered two-column layout — alternating photo + description for each service type (regular cleaning, deep clean, move-in/move-out). NOT icon cards.
- Trust signals: Row of 3 stats (e.g., "500+ Homes Cleaned", "5-Star Rated", "Fully Insured") with gentle scroll reveal
- Testimonials: 2-3 customer quotes in a clean card layout with star ratings — NOT a carousel
- CTA: Asymmetric section — bold headline left, photo right, "Get a Free Quote" button. Warm background, not a flat colored band.
- Footer: Warm and intentional — phone, service area, social links. Matches the friendly tone of the rest of the site.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Include oversized, confident headline typography in the hero that breaks the grid slightly — large enough to feel bold and trustworthy, not timid. This creates immediate authority.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warmth and friendliness throughout — footer and CTA must match the approachable energy of the hero. Don't let the design get generic toward the bottom. Hover animations should be consistent across all cards and buttons.
