PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business name: Spotless Home Cleaning
- Service type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Get customers to contact and book

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, trustworthy, and inviting — the kind of clean that feels like home. Not a sterile corporate feel, but a friendly neighborhood service that homeowners genuinely trust. Fresh and bright with a human touch.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business that needs full navigation structure with header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp whites and soft sky blues as the base — clean and airy without feeling cold. One warm accent (soft sage green or warm teal) to add life and differentiation. Avoid generic purple or turquoise.
- Typography: Friendly but professional — a rounded or soft serif for headlines (warmth, approachability), clean sans-serif for body. Generous line height, easy to read.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a staggered two-column layout with photos and text alternating. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTAs.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light photos of clean, beautiful home interiors (kitchens, living rooms, bathrooms)
- Style: Warm, natural lighting — not sterile stock photography. Homes should look lived-in and loved, not showroom-empty.
- Application: Full-bleed hero with a stunning clean kitchen or living room, section photos alongside service descriptions
- Logo: Clean, modern wordmark-style logo with a subtle sparkle or leaf/home icon. Use the media-logo skill to generate it. Should feel trustworthy and friendly, not corporate.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Gentle fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), soft hover lift on service cards (translate-y + shadow), smooth button color transitions (200ms)
- Personality: Warm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or flashy, just smooth and polished

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed photo of a beautifully clean home interior. Large, confident headline ("Your Home, Spotlessly Clean") with a subheadline and two CTAs (Get a Quote + See Our Services). Star rating / "500+ happy customers" social proof directly below.
- Services: Staggered two-column layout — photo left / text right, then text left / photo right. Show 3 services: Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out. Real descriptions, not icon cards.
- Why Choose Us: 3–4 trust signals in a horizontal band — Insured & Bonded, Eco-Friendly Products, Satisfaction Guaranteed, Flexible Scheduling. Use subtle icons + short text, NOT colored circles.
- Testimonials: 2–3 customer quotes in a warm, card-based layout with star ratings and first names.
- CTA Section: Asymmetric layout — headline on left, contact form (name, phone, message) on right. Warm background color, not a plain colored band.
- Footer: Clean multi-column footer with logo, nav links, phone number, and service area. Matches the warm, professional tone.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Staggered photo/text service layout creates editorial feel — like a home magazine, not a generic service directory. The warmth of natural photography throughout sets it apart from cold, corporate cleaning sites.

CONSISTENCY:
Warm, trustworthy tone throughout — footer and CTA section should feel as intentional as the hero. Don't let the design get generic toward the bottom.
