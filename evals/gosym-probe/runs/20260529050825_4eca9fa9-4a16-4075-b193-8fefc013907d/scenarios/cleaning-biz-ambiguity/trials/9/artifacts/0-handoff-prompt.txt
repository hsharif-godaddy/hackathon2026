PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning (solo operator)
- Primary goal: Get customers to contact / request a quote
- Tone: Simple, trustworthy, approachable

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, clean, and inviting — like a freshly cleaned home. Should feel personal and trustworthy, not corporate. Bright and airy with a sense of freshness and care. Stands out from generic cleaning company templates through warm color choices and editorial typography.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business that needs full navigation structure.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Warm whites and soft sky blue as the base, with a fresh mint or sage green accent. Feels clean and calm without being cold or clinical.
- Typography: Friendly but professional — a soft rounded serif or humanist sans for headlines, clean sans-serif for body. Warm and approachable, not corporate.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a two-column staggered card layout with short descriptions. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTAs. Use an asymmetric CTA with a photo or shape element alongside the text.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light images of clean, tidy home interiors (kitchens, living rooms, bathrooms)
- Style: Warm, natural lighting — not sterile stock photos. Feels like a real home.
- Application: Full-bleed hero image, section accents, service card backgrounds
- Logo: Simple, friendly wordmark-style logo with a subtle cleaning motif (sparkle or leaf). Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y + soft shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or fast

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed bright home interior photo, headline left-aligned with strong value prop ("A spotless home, every time"), phone number or "Get a Free Quote" CTA button prominent. Subtle fade-in entrance.
- Services: Staggered two-column layout showing 3–4 services (Standard Clean, Deep Clean, Move-In/Move-Out, etc.) with short descriptions — NOT icon cards
- Trust/Social proof: 2–3 short customer testimonials with first name and star rating
- CTA: Asymmetric section — text on left, warm photo or soft shape element on right. "Ready for a cleaner home?" with a contact button.
- Footer: Warm and simple — business name, phone, service area, social links. Matches overall warmth.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Include oversized, warm headline typography in the hero that feels personal — like a note from a neighbor, not a corporate ad. This creates immediate warmth and trust.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warm, airy feel throughout — footer and CTA must match hero energy. No cold grays or harsh contrasts. Animation timing consistent (gentle, 300–400ms).
