Build a homepage design preview for a residential house cleaning business.

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning (solo/small operation)
- Primary goal: Get potential customers to call or contact
- Tone: Warm, trustworthy, approachable

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Clean and inviting with a fresh, bright aesthetic that communicates reliability and care. Should feel like a trusted local business — personal and warm, not a corporate franchise. Crisp whites and soft greens evoke cleanliness without feeling sterile.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Fresh and clean — soft sage green as primary, warm white backgrounds, a pop of sky blue as accent. Feels naturally "clean" without being cold or clinical.
- Typography: Friendly and approachable — rounded or humanist sans-serif for headings, clean sans for body. Warm and readable.
- Layout: NO icon grids for services. Use a staggered two-column layout with real imagery. NO centered-text-on-colored-band CTA.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, airy photos of clean interiors (kitchens, living rooms), and happy homeowners
- Style: Natural lighting, warm tones, real-feeling (not overly staged stock)
- Application: Full hero with lifestyle photo, service section with room photos
- Logo: Simple wordmark logo with a small leaf or sparkle mark — clean and friendly. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y + soft shadow), smooth button color transitions (200ms)
- Personality: Warm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or flashy

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-width lifestyle photo of a bright clean home, headline left-aligned, phone number / "Get a Free Quote" CTA prominently placed. Add a small social proof badge (e.g. "100+ Happy Customers").
- Services: Staggered two-column layout — photo on one side, service description on the other. Alternate sides. NOT icon cards.
- Trust signals: Simple row of 3 trust badges (Insured & Bonded, Eco-Friendly Products, Satisfaction Guaranteed)
- Testimonials: 2-3 short customer quotes in a clean card row with star ratings
- CTA: Asymmetric section — large friendly headline left, contact button right. Warm background color, not a generic colored band.
- Footer: Warm and simple — phone, email, social links. Matches overall tone.

DIFFERENTIATION: Oversized warm headline in hero that feels personal and direct, like a neighbor recommending a service — not a corporate tagline.

CONSISTENCY: Maintain warmth and friendliness throughout. Footer should feel as considered as the hero. No design energy drop-off.

BUILD ORDER:

Phase 1 — Setup (run in parallel):
- Search for images: bright clean home interiors (kitchen, living room), happy homeowner photos, eco-friendly cleaning supplies
- Set font stack using fontTheme tool — humanist/rounded sans-serif that feels warm and approachable
- Set color scheme using colorScheme tool — soft sage green primary, warm white background, sky blue accent

Phase 2 — Structure (run in parallel, after Phase 1):
- Build header with simple navigation (Home, Services, About, Contact) and phone number prominent
- Build footer with contact info and warm branding

Phase 3 — Page (after Phase 1 & 2):
- Build homepage (index.tsx) with all sections: hero, trust badges, services (staggered), testimonials, CTA

Phase 4 — Finalize:
- Mark "Homepage" todo item as COMPLETE using the todoList tool
- Ask for design feedback using askQuestion

CRITICAL: Build index.tsx LAST after setup, header, and footer are complete.

INITIAL SCOPE: Homepage design only for review. All other pages deferred.
