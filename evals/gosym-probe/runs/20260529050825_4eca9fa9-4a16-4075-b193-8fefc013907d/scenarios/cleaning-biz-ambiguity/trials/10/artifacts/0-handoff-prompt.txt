PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business name: Shine Bright Cleaning
- Service type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Generate leads (calls/contacts)

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, trustworthy, and spotlessly clean aesthetic. Should feel like a reliable neighbor you'd invite into your home — not a corporate chain. Light and airy with a sense of freshness and care.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business site that needs full navigation structure.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp whites and soft sky blues evoking cleanliness and trust, with a warm lemon-yellow accent that feels cheerful and energetic — not cold or clinical
- Typography: Friendly but professional — approachable rounded sans-serif for headings, clean readable body text. Warm and inviting, not stiff.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use staggered two-column cards with short descriptions and a visual accent. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTA. Use an asymmetric CTA with a photo/graphic on one side.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light photos of clean interiors (kitchens, living rooms), sparkling surfaces
- Style: Warm, natural lighting; real-home feel, not sterile stock photography
- Application: Full hero with photo, section accents throughout
- Logo: Clean wordmark-style logo with a subtle shine/sparkle graphic element. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y + soft shadow), smooth button color transitions (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or jarring

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Strong headline ("Your Home, Sparkling Clean"), photo of a bright clean interior, prominent phone number / "Get a Free Quote" CTA button, and a trust strip below (e.g., "Insured & Bonded · 5-Star Rated · Same-Week Availability")
- Services: Staggered two-column layout showing service types (Standard Clean, Deep Clean, Move-In/Move-Out) with short descriptions — NOT icon grids
- Social proof: 3 customer testimonial cards with names and star ratings
- CTA: Asymmetric section — warm headline on left, photo of clean home on right, contact button below
- Footer: Maintain warmth — phone number prominent, service area note, social links

DIFFERENTIATION:
Include an oversized, bold headline in the hero that breaks the grid slightly — large enough to feel confident and local, not corporate. Pair with a warm yellow accent on the CTA button that pops against the clean white/blue palette.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warm, trustworthy energy throughout — footer and CTA must feel as polished as the hero. Don't get generic toward the bottom of the page.
