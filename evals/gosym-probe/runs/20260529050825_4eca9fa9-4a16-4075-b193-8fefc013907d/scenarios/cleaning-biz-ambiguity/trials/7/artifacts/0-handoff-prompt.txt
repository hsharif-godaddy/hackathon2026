Build a homepage design preview with this direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning (solo operator)
- Primary goal: Get customers to contact / call
- Tone: Simple, trustworthy, warm

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Clean and inviting with a fresh, airy feel. Should communicate reliability and care — like someone you'd trust in your home. Warm whites and soft greens give it a natural, clean energy without feeling clinical or corporate.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Warm off-white base with a soft sage green primary and a crisp white accent. Feels fresh and trustworthy — not sterile hospital-white, not generic blue.
- Typography: Friendly and approachable — a rounded or soft serif for headlines, clean sans-serif for body. Warm and readable.
- Layout: NO icon grids for services. Use a staggered two-column layout with before/after style imagery or lifestyle photography. Asymmetric hero with text left and a strong photo right.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light photos of clean interiors (kitchens, living rooms, sparkling surfaces)
- Style: Warm, lifestyle-oriented — real homes, not sterile stock photos
- Application: Hero photo right side, section backgrounds with subtle photo overlays
- Logo: Simple, clean wordmark-style logo with a small leaf or sparkle mark. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards, smooth button color transitions (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — nothing flashy or distracting

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Strong headline ("A cleaner home, guaranteed."), text left, bright interior photo right, phone number or "Get a Free Quote" CTA prominent
- Services: Staggered layout showing 3-4 services (Standard Clean, Deep Clean, Move-In/Move-Out, Recurring) — NOT icon grid, use cards with photos or illustrated accents
- Trust signals: Star ratings, "X happy customers", short 1-2 sentence testimonials from homeowners
- CTA: Warm, integrated section — "Ready for a spotless home?" with phone + contact button, NOT a plain colored band
- Footer: Warm and simple — business name, phone, service area, social links

DIFFERENTIATION: Oversized warm headline in the hero with a subtle hand-drawn underline accent on a key word (like "spotless" or "clean") — personal and distinctive.

CONSISTENCY: Keep the warm, trustworthy energy all the way through. Footer should feel as considered as the hero.

Phase 1 — Setup (run in parallel):
- Search for images: bright natural-light interior photos (clean kitchen, living room, sparkling bathroom)
- Set font stack using fontTheme tool — rounded/soft serif for headings, clean sans for body
- Set color scheme using colorScheme tool — warm off-white background, sage green primary, crisp white accents

Phase 2 — Structure (run in parallel, after Phase 1):
- Build header with simple navigation (Home, Services, About, Contact) and phone number
- Build footer with business name, phone, service area, and social links

Phase 3 — Page (after Phase 1 & 2):
- Build homepage (index.tsx) with all sections and animations

Phase 4 — Finalize:
