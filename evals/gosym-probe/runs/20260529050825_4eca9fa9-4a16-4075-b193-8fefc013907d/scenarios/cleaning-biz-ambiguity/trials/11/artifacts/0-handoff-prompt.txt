Build a homepage design preview for a residential house cleaning business called Sparkle Clean.

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business name: Sparkle Clean
- Service type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Get customers to contact / book a cleaning

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Fresh, bright, and trustworthy — the kind of site that makes a homeowner feel instantly confident handing over their keys. Clean whites and soft aquas create a sense of cleanliness without feeling clinical. Warm accents and real photography make it feel personal and approachable, not corporate.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full header navigation and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp white base with a soft aqua/teal primary and a warm coral or sunny yellow accent. Feels clean, fresh, and inviting — not the typical blue-gray corporate palette.
- Typography: Friendly but professional. A rounded or soft sans-serif for headings (approachable, not stiff), paired with a clean readable sans for body text. Generous line height.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a staggered two-column layout alternating image and text. DO NOT use a centered-text-on-colored-band CTA. Use an asymmetric CTA section with a large background image and overlaid text.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, airy images of clean homes, sparkling surfaces, and happy homeowners
- Style: Natural light, warm tones, real-feeling (not overly staged stock)
- Application: Full-bleed hero with overlay, alternating image/text in services section, testimonial section with soft background
- Logo: Clean, modern wordmark-style logo with a small sparkle or shine icon mark. Use the media-logo skill to generate it. Aqua/teal color with the sparkle element as the accent.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for each section (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translateY -4px + shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Warm and reassuring — motion should feel effortless, like a freshly cleaned home

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed bright home interior photo with soft overlay. Large headline ("Your Home, Spotless."), subheadline, and a prominent "Get a Free Quote" CTA button. Staggered fade-in entrance for headline, subheadline, and button.
- Services: 3 services (Regular Cleaning, Deep Clean, Move-In/Move-Out) shown in staggered two-column rows — image one side, description the other. NOT icon cards.
- Trust signals: Row of 3-4 trust badges (Insured & Bonded, Eco-Friendly Products, Satisfaction Guaranteed, 5-Star Rated) — use simple inline badges, not icon grid cards.
- Testimonials: 2-3 customer quotes in a warm-toned section. Names and star ratings.
- CTA: Asymmetric section — large background photo of a clean kitchen, overlaid with bold headline and "Book Your Cleaning" button. Not a plain colored band.
- Footer: Maintain quality — include logo, nav links, phone number, and a warm tagline.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized hero headline that breaks the grid slightly — large, confident type that immediately communicates professionalism. The staggered service layout (not icon grid) creates a premium editorial feel that most cleaning sites lack.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warmth and brightness throughout — footer and CTA should feel as polished as the hero. Do not let the design get generic toward the bottom of the page.

BUILD ORDER:

Phase 1 — Setup (run these in parallel):
- Search for images: bright airy home interior for hero, clean kitchen/bathroom for CTA section, lifestyle cleaning photos for services
- Set font stack using the fontTheme tool (rounded/soft sans for headings, clean sans for body — e.g. Nunito + Inter or Poppins + Inter)
- Set color scheme using the colorScheme tool: crisp white background, soft aqua/teal primary, warm coral or sunny yellow accent
- Generate logo using the media-logo skill: modern wordmark "Sparkle Clean" with a small sparkle/shine icon, aqua/teal color

Phase 2 — Structure (run in parallel, after Phase 1):
- Build Header with logo and navigation (Home, Services, About, Contact) — clean, minimal, white background
- Build Footer with logo, nav links, phone placeholder, and warm tagline

Phase 3 — Page (after Phase 1 & 2):
- Build homepage (index.tsx) with all sections: Hero, Services (staggered), Trust Badges, Testimonials, CTA, with scroll animations
