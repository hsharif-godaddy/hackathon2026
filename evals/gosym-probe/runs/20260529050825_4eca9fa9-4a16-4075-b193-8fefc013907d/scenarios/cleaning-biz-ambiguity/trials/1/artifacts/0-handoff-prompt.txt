PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business name: Shine Bright Cleaning
- Service type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Generate quote requests / leads

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, trustworthy, and spotless — the design should feel like a breath of fresh air. Think clean whites with warm accents that feel inviting and personal, not corporate. This is a one-person house cleaning business, so the personality should be friendly, reliable, and approachable — not a faceless franchise.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp white base with a warm sky-blue primary and a soft golden-yellow accent. Feels clean and sunny, not cold or clinical.
- Typography: Friendly but professional — a rounded sans-serif for headings (warm and approachable), clean sans for body text. Confident without being stiff.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a two-column staggered layout with illustrated or photographic cards. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTA. Use an asymmetric CTA with a large visual element on one side.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, airy photos of clean interiors (sparkling kitchen, fresh bedroom, gleaming bathroom)
- Style: Natural light, warm tones, lifestyle photography (not sterile stock)
- Application: Full-bleed hero with overlay, service section uses real room photos in cards
- Logo: Friendly wordmark-style logo with a subtle shine/sparkle graphic element. Clean, modern, trustworthy. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y -4px + shadow), smooth button color transitions (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or aggressive

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed bright interior photo with warm overlay. Large friendly headline ("Your Home, Sparkling Clean"), subheadline about reliability and care, prominent "Get a Free Quote" CTA button. Star rating / "100+ happy homes" social proof below fold.
- Services: Staggered two-column layout with room photos (kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room). Each card shows what's included — NOT icon grids.
- Why Choose Us: 3 trust pillars in a bento-style layout (Insured & Trusted, Eco-Friendly Products, Satisfaction Guaranteed) with varied card sizes.
- Testimonials: 2-3 customer quotes in a warm, editorial layout — not a generic carousel.
- CTA: Asymmetric section — large "Ready for a clean home?" headline on left, quote request form snippet or phone number on right. Warm background color.
- Footer: Warm and friendly, matches brand energy. Phone, email, service area, social links.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized warm headline typography in the hero that feels personal and inviting — like a note from a neighbor, not a corporate ad.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warm, friendly energy throughout — footer and CTA must match hero quality. Don't get generic toward the bottom.
