Build a homepage design preview for a residential house cleaning business.

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business name: Shine Bright Cleaning
- Service type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Generate leads (calls/contact)

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, trustworthy, and spotlessly fresh. Should feel like a reliable neighbor you'd invite into your home — not a cold corporate chain. Light and airy with a sense of cleanliness and care. Approachable and professional.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business website that needs full navigation structure with header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp whites and soft sky blues as the base, with a warm lemon-yellow accent that feels sunny and energetic (not neon). Conveys cleanliness without feeling clinical.
- Typography: Friendly but professional — a rounded or soft sans-serif for headings that feels approachable, clean sans for body text. Confident hierarchy without feeling corporate.
- Layout: NO icon grids for services. Use staggered two-column layout with photography and text alternating. NO centered-text-on-colored-band CTA. NO empty-right hero.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light images of clean interiors (kitchens, living rooms, sparkling surfaces)
- Style: Warm, natural lighting. Real-home feel, not sterile stock imagery.
- Application: Full hero with warm interior photo, alternating photo/text in services section, small accent photo in CTA area
- Logo: Clean, modern wordmark-style logo with a subtle shine or sparkle element. Use the media-logo skill to generate it. Place in header top-left.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Gentle fade-in on scroll for each section (350ms ease-out), hover lift on service cards (translate-y -4px + soft shadow), smooth CTA button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — motion should feel like a deep breath, not a sales pitch

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-width warm interior photo background with overlay, large headline ("Your Home, Spotlessly Clean"), subheadline, and two CTAs (Get a Free Quote + See Our Services). Staggered text entrance animation. Include a small trust badge row below (e.g., "Insured & Bonded · 5-Star Rated · 200+ Happy Homes").
- Services: Staggered two-column layout — alternating photo left/text right and text left/photo right. Show 3 services: Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning. Each with a short description and a "Learn More" link. NOT icon cards.
- Testimonials: 3 customer quote cards in a horizontal row with star ratings. Warm card backgrounds (soft cream or light blue). Staggered scroll reveal.
- CTA: Asymmetric layout — bold headline on left, contact info + button on right. Warm yellow accent background. NOT a plain centered band.
- Footer: Multi-column with nav links, contact info (phone, email), service areas, and a small tagline. Maintain warm, clean aesthetic.

DIFFERENTIATION: Oversized italic headline in the hero that breaks the grid — something like "Spotlessly Clean" in a large, slightly italic display weight that feels confident and memorable.

CONSISTENCY: Maintain warmth and quality throughout. Footer should feel as intentional as the hero. Animation easing consistent (ease-out) across all sections.

- Search for images: bright residential interior photos (clean kitchen, sparkling living room, fresh bedroom), natural warm lighting
- Generate logo using media-logo skill: clean wordmark "Shine Bright Cleaning" with subtle sparkle/shine element, sky blue and yellow palette
- Set font stack using fontTheme tool: rounded/soft sans-serif for headings (e.g. Plus Jakarta Sans or Nunito), clean sans for body

Phase 2 — Structure (run in parallel, after Phase 1):
- Build header with logo, navigation links (Home, Services, About, Contact), and a prominent "Get a Quote" CTA button
- Build footer with multi-column layout: nav links, contact info, service areas tagline

Phase 3 — Page (after Phase 1 & 2):
- Build homepage (index.tsx) with: hero, trust badges, services (staggered), testimonials, CTA section — with all animations
