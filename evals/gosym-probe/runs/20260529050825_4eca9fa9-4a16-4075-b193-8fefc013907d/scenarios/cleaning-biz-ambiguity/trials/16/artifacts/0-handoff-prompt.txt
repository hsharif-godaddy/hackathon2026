Build a homepage design preview for a residential house cleaning business called "Sparkle Clean."

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Get customers to contact and book
- Brand personality: Trustworthy, fresh, warm, approachable

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Clean and inviting with a bright, airy feel that communicates freshness and reliability. Should feel like a trusted local business — warm and personal, not a corporate chain. Generous whitespace with pops of color that feel energizing without being harsh.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business that needs full navigation structure with header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp white base with a warm sky blue as the primary accent and soft lemon yellow as a secondary highlight. Feels fresh and clean — not cold or clinical.
- Typography: Friendly but professional — a rounded or soft sans-serif for headings that feels approachable, clean sans for body. Warm and readable.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a staggered two-column layout with real imagery alongside benefit descriptions. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTA.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light images of clean, sparkling interiors (kitchens, living rooms, bathrooms)
- Style: Warm, natural-light photography showing the results of a clean home
- Application: Full hero with a beautiful clean-home image, service section photos alongside text, before/after feel
- Logo: Clean, modern wordmark-style logo with a subtle sparkle or shine element. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Gentle fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), hover lift on service cards (translate-y + soft shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Warm and reassuring — smooth, never bouncy or jarring

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-width clean home photography with headline overlay ("A Sparkling Clean Home, Every Time"), subheadline, and prominent "Get a Free Quote" CTA button. Trust badge row below (e.g., "Insured & Bonded", "5-Star Rated", "Eco-Friendly Products").
- Services: Staggered two-column layout — alternating image left/text right and text left/image right. Show 3 services: Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out. NOT icon cards.
- Testimonials: 3 customer quotes in a warm card layout with star ratings. Real names, neighborhood references (e.g., "Sarah M. — Portland").
- CTA: Asymmetric section with a warm background color (not a flat colored band). Headline + short copy + "Book Now" button, with a small home illustration or shape accent.
- Footer: Warm and intentional — logo, nav links, phone number, and a short tagline. Match the overall warmth.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Include an oversized, bold headline in the hero that breaks the grid slightly — large enough to feel confident and memorable. Pair with a trust-badge strip immediately below the hero fold.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warmth and friendliness throughout — footer and CTA must feel as polished as the hero. No design energy drop-off.
