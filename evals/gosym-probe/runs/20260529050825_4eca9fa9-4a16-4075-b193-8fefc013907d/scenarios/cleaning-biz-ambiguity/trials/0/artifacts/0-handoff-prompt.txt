Build a homepage design preview with this direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning (solo/small operator)
- Primary goal: Get potential customers to call or contact
- Tone: Simple, trustworthy, approachable

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Clean and inviting with a fresh, airy feel. Should communicate trustworthiness and reliability — the kind of person you'd feel comfortable letting into your home. Warm whites and soft greens create a sense of cleanliness without feeling clinical or corporate.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Fresh and clean — soft sage green as primary, warm off-white background, crisp white cards. Conveys cleanliness without the sterile blue/white cliché.
- Typography: Friendly but professional. Rounded or soft sans-serif for headings, clean readable body text. Warm and approachable, not corporate.
- Layout: NO icon grids for services. Use large illustrated or photo-backed service cards in an asymmetric 2-column layout. Hero should have text left, lifestyle photo right.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light photos of clean homes, sparkling surfaces, fresh interiors
- Style: Warm, inviting lifestyle photography (not sterile stock shots)
- Application: Full hero image on right side, section backgrounds with subtle soft green organic accents
- Logo: Simple, friendly wordmark with a small leaf or sparkle icon. Generate using media-logo skill. Clean, modern, trustworthy feel.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y-1 + shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or flashy

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Strong headline ("Your Home, Spotlessly Clean"), subheadline with service area mention, prominent "Get a Free Quote" CTA button, lifestyle photo of a bright clean living room on the right
- Services: 3 service cards (Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out) in a staggered 2-column layout with real descriptions — NOT icon grids
- Why Choose Us: 3 trust points (Insured & Bonded, Eco-Friendly Products, Satisfaction Guaranteed) in a warm horizontal band — use checkmarks or simple badges, not icon circles
- Testimonials: 2-3 customer quote cards with star ratings, staggered reveal on scroll
- CTA section: Asymmetric layout with a warm sage green background, "Ready for a Cleaner Home?" headline, and contact button — NOT a centered colored band
- Footer: Business name, phone placeholder, service area, and social links. Warm and consistent with page design.

DIFFERENTIATION: Oversized warm headline in the hero with generous line-height that feels personal and inviting, not corporate.

CONSISTENCY: Maintain warmth and approachability throughout — footer should feel as intentional as the hero. No design energy drop-off.
