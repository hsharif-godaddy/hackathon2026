Build a homepage design preview for a residential house cleaning business.

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning (solo/small operator)
- Primary goal: Get customers to call or book
- Audience: Homeowners looking for a trustworthy, reliable cleaner

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, fresh, and trustworthy — like a neighbor you'd actually let into your home. Clean whites and soft greens convey cleanliness without feeling clinical. Should feel personal and approachable, not corporate.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full header navigation and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp white base with soft sage green as the primary accent and a warm cream for section backgrounds. Avoid sterile hospital-blue or generic teal.
- Typography: Friendly but professional — a rounded or soft sans-serif for headings paired with a clean sans for body text. Warm and readable.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a 2-column alternating layout with illustrated or photographic cards. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTA.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, airy images of clean, sunlit interiors (kitchens, living rooms, bathrooms)
- Style: Natural light, warm tones, real-home feel (not staged hotel shots)
- Application: Full hero with a bright clean-home photo, service section photos showing specific rooms
- Logo: Simple, friendly wordmark-style logo with a small leaf or sparkle mark. Use media-logo skill to generate — do NOT substitute text or emoji.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y + soft shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Warm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or flashy

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-width bright interior photo with headline overlay ("A Spotless Home, Every Time"), subheadline, and a prominent "Get a Free Quote" CTA button. Small trust badge row below (e.g., "Insured & Bonded · Eco-Friendly Products · 5-Star Rated").
- Services: 3 service cards (Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out) using alternating layout with room photos — NOT icon cards.
- Trust/Social proof: 3 customer testimonial quotes with star ratings and first names. Simple, warm layout.
- CTA section: Asymmetric layout — headline left, phone number + button right, soft sage background. Not a centered band.
- Footer: Warm and clean — business name, nav links, phone number, and a short tagline.

DIFFERENTIATION: Oversized warm headline in the hero with a subtle hand-drawn underline accent on a key word (e.g., "Spotless"). Creates a personal, human touch that stands out from generic cleaning sites.

CONSISTENCY: Maintain warmth and approachability throughout — footer should feel as intentional as the hero. No energy drop-off.

BUILD ORDER:

Phase 1 — Setup (run in parallel):
- Search for images: bright sunlit kitchen, clean living room, sparkling bathroom, happy homeowner
- Set font stack using fontTheme tool — choose a warm, friendly sans-serif (e.g., Plus Jakarta Sans or DM Sans for headings, Inter for body)
- Set color scheme using colorScheme tool — crisp white background, sage green primary, warm cream secondary, dark charcoal text
- Generate logo using media-logo skill — friendly wordmark with small sparkle/leaf mark

Phase 2 — Structure (run in parallel, after Phase 1):
- Build header with logo + navigation (Home, Services, About, Contact) + "Book Now" CTA button
- Build footer with business name, links, phone, and tagline

Phase 3 — Page (after Phase 1 & 2):
- Build homepage (index.tsx) with all sections: hero, trust badges, services, testimonials, CTA section
- Ensure all animations are implemented

Phase 4 — Finalize:
- Mark "Homepage" todo item as complete using todoList tool
- Ask for design feedback using askQuestion

CRITICAL: Build index.tsx LAST after setup, header, and footer are complete.

INITIAL SCOPE: Homepage design only for review.

DESIGN FEEDBACK:
After showing the homepage, use askQuestion to ask:
- Question: "What do you think of the design?"
- Suggestions: ["Looks great — let's add a Services & Pricing page", "Change the color scheme", "Change the fonts", "I'd like to adjust something specific"]
