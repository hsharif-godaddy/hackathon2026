Build a homepage design preview for a residential house cleaning business.

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Get customers to call or book a cleaning
- Tone: Warm, trustworthy, local, professional

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Fresh and inviting aesthetic that feels spotless and trustworthy — like a home that's just been cleaned. Warm whites and soft teals create a sense of cleanliness without feeling cold or clinical. Should feel like a friendly local professional, not a corporate chain.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp off-white base with a warm teal/sage green accent — conveys cleanliness and freshness without the generic "blue cleaning company" cliché. Subtle warm cream undertones keep it inviting.
- Typography: Friendly and approachable — a rounded or soft serif for headlines (warmth and trust), clean sans-serif for body text. Confident but never corporate.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a staggered two-column layout with large imagery and short punchy descriptions. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTAs.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, airy photos of clean interiors (sparkling kitchen, fresh bedroom, gleaming bathroom)
- Style: Natural light, warm tones, real-home feel (not sterile stock photos)
- Application: Full-bleed hero with a beautiful clean interior, photography woven into service sections
- Logo: A clean, simple wordmark logo with a small sparkling or leaf/shine icon mark. Use the media-logo skill to generate it. Style should feel fresh and professional — not clipart.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Gentle fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), soft hover lift on service cards (translate-y + shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Clean and calm — motion should feel like a fresh breeze, never jarring

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed bright interior photo with headline overlay ("A Spotless Home, Every Time" or similar), phone number prominently displayed, and a "Get a Free Quote" CTA button. Subtle fade-in entrance.
- Services: Staggered two-column layout — image one side, service description other side. Alternate sides. NOT icon cards. Services: Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out.
- Trust signals: 3 stat callouts (e.g., "500+ Happy Clients", "5-Star Rated", "Fully Insured") in a horizontal band — use numbers, not icons.
- Testimonials: 2-3 short customer quotes in a warm-toned section with names. Real, conversational tone.
- CTA: Asymmetric layout — headline left, phone + button right. Warm teal background. Not a centered band.
- Footer: Clean and simple with business name, phone, and nav links. Matches the warm, professional tone.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized headline typography in the hero that feels confident and personal — "Your Home Deserves to Shine" in large, warm serif type. This creates immediate emotional connection vs. generic cleaning site templates.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warmth and cleanliness throughout — footer should feel as intentional as the hero. Don't let the design get generic toward the bottom.

BUILD ORDER:

Phase 1 — Setup (run in parallel):
- Search for images: bright airy clean interior for hero, sparkling kitchen, fresh bedroom, clean bathroom
- Set font stack using fontTheme tool — warm serif for headings (e.g. Playfair Display or Lora), clean sans for body (e.g. DM Sans or Nunito)
- Set color scheme using colorScheme tool — off-white background, warm teal primary accent, soft cream secondary, dark charcoal text

Phase 2 — Structure (run in parallel, after Phase 1):
- Build header with logo (use media-logo skill), business name, nav links (Home, Services, About, Contact), and a prominent phone number or "Book Now" button
- Build footer with business name, phone, nav links, and a short tagline

Phase 3 — Page (after Phase 1 & 2):
- Build homepage (index.tsx) with all sections: hero, services (staggered), trust stats, testimonials, CTA, and animations

Phase 4 — Finalize:
- Mark "Homepage" todo item as complete using todoList tool
- Ask for design feedback using askQuestion

CRITICAL: Build index.tsx LAST after setup, header, and footer are complete.

INITIAL SCOPE: Homepage design only for this preview.
