- Business type: Residential house cleaning (solo/small operator)
- Tone: Simple, warm, trustworthy — not corporate
- Primary goal: Get customers to call or book

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, fresh, and approachable — like a neighbor you trust with your home. Clean whitespace with pops of color that feel cheerful, not clinical. Should feel personal and reliable, not like a big franchise chain.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Fresh and inviting — soft sky blue as primary with warm white backgrounds and a sunny yellow-green accent. Feels clean and bright without being cold or sterile.
- Typography: Friendly and readable — a rounded sans-serif for headings (warm, approachable), clean sans for body. Confident but never stiff.
- Layout: NO icon grids for services. Use a bento-style card layout with varied sizes. Hero should have text on left with a warm lifestyle photo on the right.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light photos of clean homes, sparkling surfaces, happy homeowners
- Style: Warm, authentic lifestyle photography (not sterile stock)
- Application: Full hero image or split hero, section accents with soft rounded shapes
- Logo: Simple, friendly wordmark with a small sparkle or leaf accent mark. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Gentle fade-in on scroll for service cards (staggered, 300ms), soft hover lift on cards (translate-y + shadow), smooth CTA button color fill on hover
- Personality: Warm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or flashy

Oversized, friendly headline in the hero that feels personal — like "Your Home, Sparkling Clean." with a handwritten-style accent word or underline on "Sparkling."
