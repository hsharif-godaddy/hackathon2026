PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business name: Shine Bright Cleaning
- Service type: Residential house cleaning
- Primary goal: Get customers to contact / book a cleaning

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, trustworthy, and spotlessly fresh. Should feel like a reliable neighbor you'd invite into your home — not a cold corporate cleaning chain. Clean whites and soft warm tones convey cleanliness without feeling sterile.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business that needs full navigation structure with header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp whites and soft sky blue as the primary, with a warm lemon-yellow accent for CTAs. Feels clean, bright, and approachable — not clinical.
- Typography: Friendly and confident. Rounded or soft sans-serif for headings (e.g. Plus Jakarta Sans or DM Sans), clean readable sans for body. Warm and approachable, not corporate.
- Layout: NO icon grids for services. Use large illustrated or photo-backed cards with staggered layout. Asymmetric hero with visual balance.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, airy interior shots of clean homes (living rooms, kitchens). Natural light, warm tones.
- Style: Real-feeling lifestyle photography, not sterile stock. Sunlit rooms, fresh linens, gleaming surfaces.
- Application: Full hero background with overlay, service section with photo cards, testimonials with warm background.
- Logo: Clean, modern wordmark with a small sparkle or shine icon. Bright and friendly. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y -4px + shadow), smooth CTA button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Warm and reassuring — smooth and gentle, never bouncy or jarring

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed bright interior photo with warm overlay. Large headline "A Spotless Home, Every Time." Subhead about reliability and trust. CTA button "Get a Free Quote" in warm yellow. Star rating / social proof snippet below headline.
- Services: Staggered two-column layout with photo-backed cards (not icon grids). Services like Regular Cleaning, Deep Clean, Move-In/Move-Out. Each card has a photo, title, and short description.
- Why Choose Us: Asymmetric layout with 3 trust points (Insured & Bonded, Eco-Friendly Products, Satisfaction Guaranteed). Use large bold numbers or icons with short copy — NOT a uniform icon grid.
- Testimonials: Warm cream/yellow background section. 2-3 customer quotes with names and star ratings. Feels personal and genuine.
- CTA Section: Asymmetric layout — headline left, button right, with a subtle background texture or shape. NOT a centered colored band.
- Footer: Warm and intentional. Business name, nav links, phone/contact, and a short tagline. Matches overall warmth.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized headline typography in the hero that feels confident and bold. The warm yellow accent used sparingly as a punctuation color creates a sunny, optimistic brand feel that stands out from generic blue cleaning sites.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warmth and brightness throughout — footer and CTA must feel as intentional as the hero. Don't get generic toward the bottom of the page. Hover animations consistent across all cards.
