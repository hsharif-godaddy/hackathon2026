Build a homepage design preview with this direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning (solo operator)
- Primary goal: Generate leads — get visitors to call or contact
- Tone: Simple, trustworthy, warm, local

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Clean and inviting with a fresh, airy feel. Should communicate reliability and care — like a neighbor you trust, not a corporate chain. Warm whites and soft greens create a sense of cleanliness without feeling clinical.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Fresh and clean — soft sage green as primary accent against warm white backgrounds. Avoid sterile hospital whites; use warm off-whites (cream). Muted teal for secondary accents.
- Typography: Friendly and approachable — a rounded or soft serif for headings (warmth), clean sans-serif for body. Not corporate, not playful — just trustworthy.
- Layout: NO icon grids for services. Use a staggered two-column layout with photos alongside service descriptions. Asymmetric hero with strong headline and photo.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light photos of clean, tidy rooms (kitchens, living rooms, bathrooms)
- Style: Warm, natural lighting; real-feeling spaces, not overly staged
- Application: Hero image of a beautifully clean kitchen or living room, service section photos, atmosphere shots
- Logo: Simple, clean wordmark-style logo with a small leaf or sparkle icon in sage green. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), gentle hover lift on service cards (translate-y + soft shadow), smooth button color transitions (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or flashy

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Strong headline ("A cleaner home, without the hassle"), subheadline, prominent "Get a Free Quote" CTA button. Photo of a sparkling clean room on the right. Fade-in entrance animation.
- Services: 3 services (Standard Clean, Deep Clean, Move-In/Move-Out) shown as staggered cards with a photo or illustration — NOT icon grids. Scroll-triggered reveal.
- Why choose us: 3 trust points (Insured & bonded, Eco-friendly products, Satisfaction guaranteed) in a horizontal row — simple and clean, not icon-heavy.
- Testimonials: 2–3 short customer quotes in a warm, card-based layout with star ratings.
- CTA section: Asymmetric layout with a warm background color, "Ready for a spotless home?" headline, and a phone number + contact button. Not a centered colored band.
- Footer: Clean and simple — business name, phone, email, social links. Matches the warm, friendly tone.

DIFFERENTIATION: Oversized, confident headline in the hero that breaks the typical small-text pattern. Warm cream background tones instead of stark white throughout.

CONSISTENCY: Maintain the warm, trustworthy feel all the way through the footer. No design energy drop-off.
