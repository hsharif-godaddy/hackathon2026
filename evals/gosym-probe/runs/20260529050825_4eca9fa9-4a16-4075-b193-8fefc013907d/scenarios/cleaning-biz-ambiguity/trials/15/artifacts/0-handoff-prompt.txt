PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Residential house cleaning
- Goal: Get customers to contact/book
- Tone: Simple, trustworthy, warm

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Clean and inviting with a fresh, airy feel — like a home that's just been cleaned. Warm whites and soft greens create a sense of freshness and trust without feeling clinical. Should feel personal and approachable, not corporate.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
This is a local service business that needs full navigation structure with header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Crisp whites and soft off-whites as the base, with a fresh sage or mint green as the primary accent. Warm, not sterile. Conveys cleanliness without feeling like a hospital.
- Typography: Friendly and approachable — a soft rounded sans-serif for headings, clean readable sans for body. Warm and trustworthy, not stiff.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids for services. Instead use a staggered two-column layout with large imagery and overlapping text cards. DO NOT use centered-text-on-colored-band CTAs.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — bright, natural-light photos of clean, beautiful home interiors (kitchens, living rooms, bathrooms)
- Style: Warm, natural lighting; real homes feel, not staged stock photos
- Application: Full-bleed hero with a bright clean interior, alternating image/text sections for services
- Logo: Simple, clean wordmark logo with a small leaf or sparkle icon. Fresh and friendly. Use the media-logo skill to generate it.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Gentle fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), soft hover lift on service cards (translate-y-1 + shadow), smooth button color fill on hover (200ms)
- Personality: Calm and reassuring — nothing bouncy or flashy

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed bright interior photo, headline left-aligned with a warm overlay, phone number or "Get a Free Quote" CTA prominently placed. Small trust badge (e.g. "Insured & Trusted") below headline.
- Services: Staggered layout — 3 services (Regular Cleaning, Deep Clean, Move-In/Move-Out) shown with photos and short descriptions. NOT icon cards.
- Testimonials: 2-3 short customer quotes in warm card style with star ratings. Feels personal, not corporate.
- CTA: Asymmetric section — large headline on left, contact form or phone/email on right. Warm background, not a colored band.
- Footer: Warm and clean — business name, phone, email, quick links. Matches the friendly tone.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized, warm headline typography in the hero that feels personal — like a neighbor recommending their cleaner, not a corporate ad.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain the warm, airy feel throughout. Footer should feel as intentional as the hero. Keep the sage green accent consistent across buttons, highlights, and decorative elements.

- Set color scheme using the colorScheme tool — crisp white background, sage/mint green primary accent, warm gray text
- Generate logo using the media-logo skill: simple clean wordmark "Sparkling Clean" with a small sparkle or leaf icon, fresh and friendly style
