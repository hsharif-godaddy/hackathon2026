Build a full multi-page website for "Artisan Goods Co.", a handmade crafts shop. Full details below.

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business: Artisan Goods Co. — handmade crafts shop
- Categories: Pottery, Textiles, Woodwork, Jewelry
- Audience: Craft lovers, gift shoppers, design-conscious consumers
- Goal: Showcase products, tell the brand story, capture newsletter leads

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, artisanal aesthetic that feels handcrafted and authentic — like a well-curated boutique market stall brought online. Rich earth tones, natural textures, and generous whitespace. NOT a generic e-commerce template — this should feel personal and crafted.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Multi-page marketing/shop site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Warm earth palette — terracotta, warm cream/off-white, deep walnut brown, sage green accent. Nothing cold or corporate.
- Typography: Serif display font for headings (warmth, craft heritage feel), clean sans-serif for body. Generous line-height.
- Layout: Asymmetric hero with full-bleed imagery. Product grid with subtle hover lifts. NO uniform icon grids.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — search for artisan/handmade craft product photos (pottery, textiles, woodwork, jewelry)
- Style: Warm natural lighting, tactile textures, close-up detail shots
- Application: Full-bleed hero, product cards, team profile photos on About page
- Logo: Generate a logo using the media-logo skill — wordmark style with a small hand-drawn craft icon (e.g. a simple pottery wheel or leaf motif), warm terracotta/brown color

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for sections (350ms ease-out), hover lift on product cards (translate-y -4px + shadow), smooth nav link underline draw on hover
- Personality: Warm and unhurried — nothing snappy or corporate

SECTION-SPECIFIC:

Hero (Homepage):
- Full-bleed background image of artisan at work or product flatlay
- Headline: "Handcrafted with Heart" — large serif, overlaid on image with warm overlay
- Subheadline and CTA button ("Shop the Collection" → /products)
- Subtle fade-in entrance animation

Featured Products (Homepage):
- Section title "Our Favorites" in serif
- 4 featured product cards in a 2x2 or horizontal scroll on mobile
- Each card: product image, name, category tag, price
- Hover: lift effect + subtle shadow

Newsletter Signup (Homepage):
- Warm-toned band section (terracotta or deep brown background)
- Headline: "Join Our Community"
- Email input + Subscribe button
- NOT a centered-on-band generic CTA — use asymmetric layout with decorative element

Product Catalog Page (/products):
- Page title + breadcrumb
- Category filter tabs: All | Pottery | Textiles | Woodwork | Jewelry
- Products fetched from /api/products (with optional ?category= query param)
- Responsive grid: 3 cols desktop, 2 cols tablet, 1 col mobile
- Product card: image, name, category badge, price
- Loading skeleton state while fetching
- Filter updates the displayed products (client-side filter or re-fetch)

About Page (/about):
- Hero section: "Our Story" with a warm full-bleed image
- Brand story paragraph (2-3 paragraphs about handmade craft values)
- Mission statement in a pull-quote style (large serif, centered, terracotta accent)
- Team section: 3 team member cards with photo, name, role, short bio
- Staggered fade-in on scroll for team cards

Shared Header:
- Logo left, nav links right: Home | Shop | About
- Active link indicator (underline or color)
- Mobile hamburger menu with slide-in drawer
- Warm cream/off-white background, walnut brown text

Shared Footer:
- 3-column: Brand blurb + logo | Quick links | Newsletter mini-signup or social links
- Warm dark background (deep walnut/charcoal), cream text
- Maintain design quality — not an afterthought

DIFFERENTIATION:
Asymmetric hero with large serif headline breaking the grid. Product cards use a warm paper-texture-inspired card background. Pull-quote mission statement on About page creates a memorable typographic moment.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warm earth-tone energy throughout all pages and the footer. Animation timing consistent (subtle, 300-400ms). Serif/sans pairing used consistently across all pages.

Phase 4 — Pages (after Phase 2 & 3):
