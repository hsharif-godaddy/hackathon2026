Build a multi-page artisan crafts website with this design direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business: Artisan Goods Co. — handmade crafts shop
- Categories: Pottery, Textiles, Woodwork, Jewelry
- Pages: Homepage, Product Catalog, About

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm, earthy, handcrafted aesthetic that feels like stepping into a boutique maker's market. Organic textures, generous whitespace, and editorial typography that honors the handmade nature of the products. NOT a generic e-commerce template — this should feel curated and artisanal.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Multi-page site with full navigation header and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Warm neutrals — cream/off-white base, warm terracotta accent, deep walnut brown for text. Avoid pure white or cold grays.
- Typography: Serif display font for headings (warmth, craft heritage), clean sans-serif for body. Mix weights confidently — oversized hero headline.
- Layout: Full-bleed hero with editorial feel. Product grid with generous spacing. NO uniform icon grids.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — artisan product photos with natural lighting, warm tones
- Style: Authentic handmade product shots, close-up textures, natural materials
- Application: Hero uses a full-bleed craft/product image; product cards use individual product photos; team section uses portrait photos
- Logo: Wordmark-style logo with a subtle craft/leaf/hand icon. Use media-logo skill to generate.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for product cards (staggered, 300ms), gentle hover lift on product cards (translate-y -4px + shadow), smooth underline hover on nav links
- Personality: Warm and unhurried — nothing fast or jarring

SECTION-SPECIFIC:

Hero (Homepage):
- Full-bleed background image of artisan workspace or beautiful handmade goods
- Oversized serif headline ("Handcrafted with Heart") left-aligned
- Short tagline and two CTAs: "Shop Now" → /products, "Our Story" → /about
- Subtle fade-in entrance animation

Featured Products (Homepage):
- Section title: "Featured Pieces"
- Show 4 featured products in a 2x2 or 4-column grid
- Each card: product image, name, category badge, price
- Hover lift effect on cards
- "View All" link to /products

Newsletter Signup (Homepage):
- Warm, integrated section (NOT a generic colored band)
- Asymmetric layout with decorative element
- Email input + "Subscribe" button
- Copy: "Join our community of craft lovers"

Product Catalog (/products):
- Category filter bar at top: All | Pottery | Textiles | Woodwork | Jewelry
- Active filter highlighted with terracotta accent
- Product grid: responsive 2-col mobile, 3-col tablet, 4-col desktop
- Each card: image, name, category, price
- Products fetched from /api/products (with optional ?category= query param)
- Staggered fade-in as products load

About Page (/about):
- Hero section: brand story headline + atmospheric image
- Mission statement in large, elegant typography (pull-quote style)
- "Our Story" narrative section (2-col: text left, image right)
- Team profiles: 3-column grid, each with photo, name, role, short bio
- Warm, editorial feel throughout

Header:
- Logo left, nav links right: Home | Shop | About
- Clean, minimal — warm background (cream/off-white), not stark white
- Sticky on scroll with subtle shadow
- Mobile hamburger menu

Footer:
- Multi-column: Brand blurb | Quick Links | Categories | Newsletter teaser
- Warm dark background (walnut brown), cream text
- Social icons
- Copyright line

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized serif headline in the hero that breaks the grid. Product cards with warm cream backgrounds (not white) and terracotta category badges. The newsletter section uses an asymmetric layout with a decorative botanical/craft motif.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain warm, artisanal energy throughout all pages and sections. Footer should feel as intentional as the hero. Terracotta accent used consistently for CTAs, active states, and badges.

BUILD ORDER:

Phase 1 — Setup (run in parallel):
- Search for images: artisan workspace hero, pottery products, textile products, woodwork products, jewelry products, team portrait photos (3), about page atmospheric image
- Set font stack using fontTheme tool: serif display (e.g. Playfair Display or Lora) + clean sans (e.g. Inter or DM Sans)
- Set color scheme using colorTheme tool: cream background, terracotta primary, walnut brown foreground
- Create the Products API endpoint at src/server/api/products.ts with mock product data (12+ products across 4 categories: pottery, textiles, woodwork, jewelry). Each product: id, name, category, price, description, imageUrl, featured (boolean). Register it in src/server/entry.ts at GET /api/products (supports ?category= filter and returns featured products if ?featured=true).

Phase 2 — Structure (run in parallel, after Phase 1):
- Build Header (src/layouts/parts/Header.tsx): sticky, cream background, logo left, nav right (Home/Shop/About), mobile menu
- Build Footer (src/layouts/parts/Footer.tsx): dark walnut background, multi-column layout, social icons, copyright

Phase 3 — Pages (after Phase 1 & 2):
- Add routes to src/routes.tsx: / (Homepage), /products (Catalog), /about (About)
- Build Homepage (src/pages/index.tsx): hero, featured products (fetched from /api/products?featured=true via loader), newsletter signup
- Build Product Catalog (src/pages/products.tsx): category filters, product grid (fetched from /api/products via loader, supports category filtering client-side)
- Build About page (src/pages/about.tsx): story, mission statement, team profiles
