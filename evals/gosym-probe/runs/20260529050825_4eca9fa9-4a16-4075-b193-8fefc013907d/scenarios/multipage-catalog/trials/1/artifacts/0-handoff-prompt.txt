Build a multi-page handmade crafts website for "Artisan Goods Co." with this design direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business type: Handmade crafts shop (pottery, textiles, woodwork, jewelry)
- Target audience: Consumers who value artisan, handcrafted goods — likely 28–55, design-conscious
- Brand personality: Warm, earthy, artisanal, premium-but-approachable

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm and editorial with an artisan market feel. Rich earthy tones, generous whitespace, and tactile-feeling typography. Should feel like a curated boutique, not a generic e-commerce template. Photography-forward with natural textures.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Multi-page site with shared header and footer navigation.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Warm neutrals — warm cream/off-white background, deep terracotta or warm clay as primary accent, warm charcoal for text. Avoid pure white or cold grays.
- Typography: Elegant serif for headings (warmth, craft heritage), clean sans-serif for body and UI. Mix weights confidently.
- Layout: Full-bleed hero with editorial feel. Product grid with generous spacing. NO icon grids for features.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography (product shots, artisan at work, natural textures)
- Style: Warm, natural lighting — earthy tones, wood surfaces, clay textures
- Application: Hero full-bleed image, product cards with square/portrait images, team photos on About page
- Logo: Wordmark-style logo with a small craft icon (leaf, hand, or loom motif) — use the media-logo skill to generate it

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for product cards (staggered, 300ms), gentle hover lift on product cards (translate-y -4px + shadow), smooth nav link underline draw on hover
- Personality: Warm and unhurried — nothing fast or jarring

PAGES TO BUILD:

1. **Homepage** (`src/pages/index.tsx`):
   - Hero: Full-bleed image, large serif headline ("Crafted with Care"), subheadline, CTA button linking to /products
   - Featured Products: Pull 4 featured products from the API, display in a 2x2 or 4-column grid with hover lift effect
   - Newsletter signup: Warm, integrated section (NOT a centered-on-band generic CTA) — asymmetric layout with a small illustration or texture element

2. **Product Catalog** (`src/pages/products.tsx`):
   - Category filter bar: Horizontal pill/tab filters for All, Pottery, Textiles, Woodwork, Jewelry
   - Product grid: Responsive 3-column grid (2 on tablet, 1 on mobile) with product cards showing image, name, category tag, and price
   - Products fetched from `/api/products?category=...` with loading skeleton states
   - Staggered fade-in animation on product cards

3. **About Page** (`src/pages/about.tsx`):
   - Hero/banner: Brand story headline with a warm background texture
   - Mission statement: Large pull-quote style, prominent typography
   - Our Story: 2-column layout (text left, image right)
   - Team section: Grid of team member cards with photo, name, and role. Use Getty images for team photos.

PRODUCTS API (`src/server/api/products.ts`):
- Register in `src/server/entry.ts`
- GET `/api/products` — returns all products, supports `?category=` filter
- Seed with 12 realistic handmade products across 4 categories (pottery, textiles, woodwork, jewelry)
- Each product: `{ id, name, category, price, description, image, featured }`
- Use Getty stock image URLs for product images (search via media tool)

SHARED HEADER (`src/layouts/parts/Header.tsx`):
- Style: Simple/editorial — logo left, nav links right (Home, Products, About)
- Warm background (cream/off-white), terracotta accent on active/hover states
- Mobile: hamburger menu with smooth sheet/drawer open

SHARED FOOTER (`src/layouts/parts/Footer.tsx`):
- Multi-column: Brand tagline left, nav links center, social icons right
- Warm charcoal background, cream text
- Maintain design quality — not an afterthought

ROUTES (`src/routes.tsx`):
Add routes for `/`, `/products`, `/about`. Products page should use a route loader to fetch initial product data server-side.

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-bleed with warm overlay (gray-900/50), serif headline, CTA button in terracotta
- Product cards: Square image top, name + category tag + price below, hover lift animation
- Newsletter: Asymmetric layout, NOT centered text on colored band
- Team cards: Portrait photo, name in serif, role in sans-serif muted

DIFFERENTIATION: Editorial typography scale — oversized serif headings that feel like a magazine spread, not a generic shop.

CONSISTENCY: Maintain warm, artisanal energy throughout all pages and into the footer. Animation timing consistent (subtle, 300ms ease-out).
