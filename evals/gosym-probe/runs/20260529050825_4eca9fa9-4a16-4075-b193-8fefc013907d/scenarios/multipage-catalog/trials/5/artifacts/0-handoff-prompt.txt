Build a multi-page artisan crafts website. Full details below.

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Business: Artisan Goods Co. — handmade crafts shop
- Pages: Homepage, Product Catalog, About
- Audience: Craft lovers, gift shoppers, design-conscious consumers
- Tone: Warm, artisanal, trustworthy, community-rooted

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Warm and earthy with an editorial handcrafted feel. Think independent boutique, not mass-market retail. Rich textures implied through color and typography — generous whitespace, natural warmth, and confident type hierarchy that feels hand-selected, not templated.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Multi-page marketing + shop site. Full header with navigation and footer with links across all pages.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Warm neutrals — cream/off-white background, warm terracotta or clay as primary accent, deep walnut brown for text. Avoid pure white or cold grays.
- Typography: Serif display font for headings (warmth, craft heritage), clean sans-serif for body and UI. Mix weights confidently — oversized hero headline, tight leading on cards.
- Layout: Full-bleed hero with editorial feel. Product grid uses varied card proportions. NO uniform icon grids anywhere.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Photography — product shots with natural lighting, warm tones
- Style: Artisanal product photography feel — close-up textures, earthy backgrounds
- Application: Hero full-bleed image, product card images, team photos on About page
- Logo: Wordmark-style logo with a small craft icon (leaf, hand, or loom motif). Use the media-logo skill to generate it. Place in header top-left.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Fade-in on scroll for product cards (staggered, 300ms), hover lift on product cards (translate-y -4px + shadow), smooth underline draw on nav links, gentle button color fill on hover
- Personality: Warm and unhurried — nothing snappy or corporate

SECTION-SPECIFIC:

Hero (Homepage):
- Full-bleed background image (search for artisan workshop / handmade crafts hero)
- Warm overlay gradient (not pure black — use warm brown/terracotta)
- Large serif headline: "Handcrafted with Care, Made to Last"
- Subtext + two CTAs: "Shop Now" → /products, "Our Story" → /about
- Staggered entrance animation on headline + subtext + CTAs

Featured Products (Homepage):
- Section title: "Featured Pieces"
- 4 product cards pulled from /api/products?featured=true
- Asymmetric bento-style layout (2 large + 2 small cards), NOT a uniform 4-column grid
- Each card: image, category tag, product name, price, hover lift effect

Newsletter Signup (Homepage):
- Warm terracotta band — asymmetric layout (headline left, form right)
- Headline: "Join Our Community"
- Email input + "Subscribe" button
- NOT a centered-text-on-colored-band layout

Product Catalog Page (/products):
- Page title: "Our Collection"
- Category filter tabs: All | Pottery | Textiles | Woodwork | Jewelry
- Filters are client-side — filter the products array by category
- Products fetched from /api/products via route loader
- Responsive grid: 3 columns desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile
- Each card: product image, category badge, name, price, "View Details" hover reveal
- Staggered fade-in on cards when category changes

About Page (/about):
- Hero: Full-width warm image (search for artisan workshop interior)
- Mission statement section: Large serif quote-style text, centered, generous padding
- "Our Story" section: Two-column — text left, image right (asymmetric)
- Team section: 3 team member cards with photo, name, role, short bio
- Search for team member portrait photos (warm, natural lighting)

SHARED HEADER:
- Logo top-left (media-logo skill)
- Nav links: Home | Shop | About
- CTA button: "Shop Now" → /products (terracotta accent)
- Mobile hamburger menu
- Subtle bottom border, warm background matching page

SHARED FOOTER:
- Multi-column: Brand column (logo + tagline), Quick Links, Categories, Newsletter teaser
- Warm dark background (deep walnut/charcoal-brown), light text
- Social icons row
- Copyright line
- Maintain design quality — footer is NOT an afterthought

DIFFERENTIATION:
Bento-style featured products layout on homepage breaks the standard uniform grid. Serif + oversized typography in hero creates editorial, magazine-quality feel.

CONSISTENCY:
Warm earthy palette and serif/sans type pairing must carry through all three pages and the footer. Animation timing consistent (300ms ease-out). Never let the design get safer or more generic as pages progress.

BUILD ORDER:

Phase 3 — Pages (after Phase 1 & 2):

Phase 4 — Routes + Finalize:
