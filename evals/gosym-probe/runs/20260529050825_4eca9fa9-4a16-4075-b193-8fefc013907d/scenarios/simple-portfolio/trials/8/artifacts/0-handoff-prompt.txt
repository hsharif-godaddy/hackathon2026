Build a homepage design preview with this direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Type: Personal portfolio website
- Sections: Hero, About, Contact
- Design: Clean, modern, well-spaced

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Editorial and confident—a portfolio that feels like a designer/developer who has taste. Not a generic template. Strong typographic hierarchy with generous whitespace and one unexpected accent color that creates personality without overwhelming.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing/portfolio site with full navigation structure.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Near-black base (#0f0f0f or deep charcoal) with warm off-white text, and ONE warm accent (amber/gold or soft coral—NOT blue or purple). Feels sophisticated and personal.
- Typography: Mix a bold serif or high-contrast display font for the hero name (something like Playfair Display or DM Serif Display) with a clean geometric sans (like DM Sans or Plus Jakarta Sans) for body. Creates warmth and editorial feel.
- Layout: DO NOT use centered text with a button below as the hero. Instead, use a left-aligned typographic hero with the name displayed at oversized scale (breaking the grid). About section uses a two-column asymmetric layout. Contact section is minimal and elegant—NOT a form-heavy band.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Typographic—the name itself is the hero visual
- Style: Minimal decorative elements; a subtle geometric accent line or shape near the hero name
- Application: Thin horizontal rule or bracket-style accent near the name; subtle dot or line pattern as section divider
- Logo: No logo needed—name in header as wordmark

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Hero name fades and slides up on load (400ms, ease-out), staggered entrance for tagline and CTA link (150ms delay each), section content fades in on scroll with viewport trigger
- Personality: Refined and deliberate—nothing bouncy, smooth ease-out curves throughout

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-viewport height, left-aligned. Oversized name (display size, possibly multi-line). Short tagline below in muted tone. A subtle "scroll down" indicator or arrow. Animated entrance.
- About: Two-column asymmetric layout—left column has a short bold label ("About Me" in small caps or overline style), right column has 2-3 paragraphs of placeholder background text. Add a subtle skill tags row at the bottom.
- Contact: Clean and minimal. Large heading "Let's work together." followed by an email link styled prominently (not a button—an underlined link with hover animation). Social media icons (GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X) in a row below, with hover color transitions. NO contact form.
- Header: Minimal—name/wordmark left, nav links right (About, Contact). Transparent over hero, solid on scroll.
- Footer: Single line—copyright and a small "back to top" link. Matches dark aesthetic.

DIFFERENTIATION:
The hero name should be displayed at a scale that feels almost too large—breaking the expected grid. This typographic boldness is the signature element that makes it memorable.

CONSISTENCY:
Dark, editorial tone throughout. The warm accent appears on hover states, the email link, and subtle decorative elements—used sparingly so it pops. Footer matches hero energy.
