Build a homepage design preview for Marcus Webb's personal portfolio with this direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Name: Marcus Webb
- Profession: UX/UI Designer
- Aesthetic: Minimal & refined

DESIGN DIRECTION:
A quietly confident portfolio that lets the work and personality speak through restraint. Generous whitespace, deliberate typography hierarchy, and a monochrome-leaning palette with one warm accent. Should feel like a premium design studio — not a template.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Marketing/portfolio site with full navigation structure.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Near-white background (#FAFAF8 warm off-white), near-black text (#111), with ONE warm accent — a muted terracotta or dusty gold used sparingly on hover states, underlines, and the CTA. No gradients, no purple.
- Typography: Pair a refined serif (Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) for the hero name/headline with a clean geometric sans (DM Sans or Outfit) for body and nav. Large, confident type scale in the hero — name displayed oversized. Generous line-height and letter-spacing throughout.
- Layout: Asymmetric, editorial. DO NOT use centered-everything layouts. Left-aligned content with deliberate negative space on the right. Thin horizontal rules as section dividers.
- Visual elements: No stock photography. Use abstract geometric accents — a single thin circle or arc drawn in CSS/SVG near the hero, subtle and decorative. The design itself IS the visual.
- Motion: Subtle — elegant fade-in on page load for hero text (staggered, 400ms each), underline-draw hover effects on nav links and the email CTA, gentle fade-in on scroll for about and contact sections.

LOGO:
Use the media-logo skill to generate a minimal wordmark logo for "MW" — monogram style, clean and geometric. Place in header top-left.

SECTION-SPECIFIC:

Hero:
- Full-viewport-height section, left-aligned
- Oversized display of "Marcus Webb" — large enough to feel editorial (think 80–120px on desktop)
- Tagline below: "UX/UI Designer — crafting digital experiences with intention"
- Thin decorative arc/circle SVG element positioned to the right, partially off-screen
- Staggered fade-in entrance animation on name, tagline, and CTA
- Subtle "Scroll" indicator at bottom

About:
- Two-column layout: left has a short section label ("About" in small caps, muted), right has 2–3 paragraphs of placeholder bio text about Marcus's background in UX/UI design, research, and systems thinking
- Thin top border rule separating from hero
- Fade-in on scroll

Contact:
- Left-aligned, minimal
- Large clickable email: hello@marcuswebb.com styled as a prominent text link with underline-draw hover animation
- Social icons row below (LinkedIn, Dribbble, Twitter/X) — icon-only, clean, with warm accent hover color
- Short line: "Available for freelance & full-time opportunities"
- Thin top border rule

Footer:
- Single line: "© 2026 Marcus Webb" left-aligned, "Designed with intention" right-aligned
- Matches the refined tone — no heavy footer block

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Staggered fade-up on hero text (name → tagline → CTA, 400ms each, ease-out), fade-in on scroll for about/contact sections (viewport once: true), underline-draw on nav and email hover (CSS transition, 200ms), warm accent color shift on social icon hover
- Personality: Slow, deliberate, elegant — never bouncy or playful

Oversized left-aligned hero typography that breaks the expected centered portfolio layout. The name is the visual. Thin decorative SVG arc adds depth without photography.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain editorial restraint throughout. Footer as intentional as hero. Warm accent used consistently — only on interactive hover states. No section should feel busier than the last.
