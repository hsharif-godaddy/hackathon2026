Build a personal portfolio homepage with this design direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Type: Personal portfolio website
- Sections: Hero, About, Contact
- Design goal: Clean, modern, well-spaced — distinctive without being loud

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Refined and editorial — a portfolio that feels like a thoughtful designer/developer's personal brand. Generous whitespace, confident typography, and a monochrome-leaning palette with one warm accent. Should feel premium and intentional, not like a generic template.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Multi-page site with full header/footer navigation.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Near-black base (#0f0f0f or deep charcoal) with off-white text and ONE warm accent (amber/gold or soft coral — not blue, not purple). Sections alternate between dark and light backgrounds for rhythm.
- Typography: Serif display font for the hero name (elegant, editorial weight) paired with a clean geometric sans-serif for body. Large size contrast between heading and body.
- Layout: DO NOT use icon grids or centered-text-on-band CTAs. Use asymmetric compositions — name large and left-anchored in hero, about section with a two-column layout (text left, visual/decorative element right), contact section with generous spacing and inline social links.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Primary: Geometric typographic treatment — oversized initials or name as a background design element in the hero
- Style: Minimal decorative elements — thin lines, subtle grid accents, geometric shapes in the accent color
- Application: Hero uses large typographic composition; about section uses a subtle geometric accent shape; contact section is clean and spacious
- Logo: Use the media-logo skill to generate a minimal wordmark logo for the header

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects:
  - Hero name fades and slides up on load (500ms, easeOut)
  - Tagline fades in with a slight delay (200ms after name)
  - About and Contact sections fade in on scroll (viewport: once: true, 400ms)
  - Hover states on social icons: scale(1.1) + accent color shift (150ms)
  - Nav links: subtle underline draw on hover
- Personality: Calm and confident — smooth, never bouncy

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-viewport height. Name displayed very large (display size, serif). Tagline below in smaller sans-serif. Subtle geometric accent or oversized initial letter as background decoration. CTA arrow or "scroll down" indicator. Entrance animation on load.
- About: Two-column layout — left has bio text with a section label ("About Me" in small caps or muted label style), right has a placeholder profile image area or decorative geometric shape. NOT a centered paragraph block.
- Contact: Spacious section. Email as a large, styled link (not a button). Social icons (GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Dribbble) as inline icon row with hover effects. Short intro line above. No form needed — just direct links.
- Header: Minimal — logo/name left, nav links right (About, Contact). Clean and unobtrusive.
- Footer: Minimal — copyright line and a small "back to top" link. Matches the dark aesthetic.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized serif name in the hero that nearly fills the viewport width — typographic as art. This creates immediate impact and memorability without relying on photography.

CONSISTENCY:
Maintain editorial quality throughout. The contact section should feel as considered as the hero. Animation easing should be consistent (easeOut) across all scroll reveals.
