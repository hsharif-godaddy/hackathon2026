Build a personal portfolio homepage with this design direction:

PROJECT DETAILS:
- Type: Personal portfolio website
- Sections: Hero, About, Contact
- Design: Clean, modern, well-spaced

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Sophisticated minimal portfolio with strong typographic hierarchy and purposeful whitespace. Should feel like a premium personal brand — confident and refined, not a generic template. One unexpected element: an oversized name treatment in the hero that breaks the grid.

LAYOUT TYPE: Standard
Full header with navigation and footer.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Color: Near-black base (#0f0f0f or very dark charcoal) with a warm off-white for body text, and ONE warm accent — a muted amber/gold (#c9a84c or similar) used sparingly for highlights and hover states. Not the typical blue or purple.
- Typography: Pair a high-contrast serif (like Playfair Display or DM Serif Display) for the hero name with a clean geometric sans (like DM Sans or Plus Jakarta Sans) for body text. The contrast between the two creates instant personality.
- Layout: Asymmetric hero with oversized name spanning full width, tagline offset below. About section uses a two-column layout (text left, visual/decorative element right). Contact section is minimal and elegant — NOT a centered band.
- Visual elements: Subtle geometric line accents (thin horizontal rules, small decorative marks) in the accent color. No stock photography needed — the typography IS the visual.
- Motion: Subtle — staggered fade-in entrance on hero text, gentle underline draw on nav hover, smooth section fade-in on scroll.

MOTION DESIGN:
- Level: Subtle
- Effects: Hero name fades in with slight upward drift (500ms, easeOut), tagline follows with 150ms delay, nav links get elegant underline-draw hover (200ms), sections fade in on scroll (400ms, once: true)
- Personality: Refined and deliberate — nothing bouncy or playful

SECTION-SPECIFIC:
- Hero: Full-viewport height. Oversized name (display size, grid-breaking). Short tagline below, offset to the right. Subtle scroll indicator at bottom. Staggered entrance animation.
- About: Two-column — left has bio text with generous line height, right has a decorative typographic element or abstract geometric shape in accent color. NOT a photo placeholder.
- Contact: Clean, open layout. Large "Get in touch" heading, email as a styled link (not a button), social icons (GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X) as minimal icon links with hover color shift to accent. No form needed.
- Header: Minimal — name/initials as logo on left, sparse nav (About, Contact) on right. Transparent over hero, solid on scroll.
- Footer: Single line, copyright only. Matches dark aesthetic.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Oversized hero name that spans the full width — large enough to feel architectural, not just typographic. This creates immediate visual impact and signals design sensibility.

CONSISTENCY:
Dark, refined aesthetic throughout. The warm accent color appears consistently as the "signature" color — hover states, decorative marks, active nav. Footer maintains the same dark tone as the hero.
