Our Story

Three generations of the same recipe

Marco Russo brought this recipe from Naples in 1987. The dough is still fermented for 48 hours. The oven still burns oak and cherry wood. Nothing has changed — because nothing needed to.

In Chicago since 1987
48-hour cold-fermented dough
800°F oak and cherry wood oven
