From Naples to Your Table

Marco De Luca grew up in Napoli, where pizza is a religion. His grandfather ran a pizzeria near the Spaccanapoli for 40 years. When Marco moved to Austin in 2014, he brought the family recipes — and a 1,200-lb Stefano Ferrara oven.

Everything is made in-house: the dough ferments for 48 hours, the sauce is uncooked San Marzano, and the cheese comes directly from a caseificio in Campania. There are no shortcuts here.
