Sourdoughs, croissants, and hand-crafted pastries made with locally sourced ingredients and 12 years of passion.

Ben's Bakery started as a weekend project in a tiny apartment kitchen. Today, we bake over 200 loaves a day using the same slow-fermentation recipes Ben perfected over a decade. Every loaf is shaped by hand, proofed overnight, and baked in our stone-deck oven starting at 4am.

Our Story

Ben Kowalski learned to bake from his grandmother in Kraków, Poland. Her sourdough starter — now over 80 years old — lives in our kitchen and goes into every loaf we make.

We believe great bread takes time. Our doughs ferment for 18–24 hours before they ever see the oven. That slow process develops flavor, texture, and the kind of crust you can hear across the room when you break it.

Every ingredient is sourced within 150 miles of Austin: whole-grain flour from Hill Country mills, local honey, Texas pecans, and seasonal fruit from our neighbors at the farmers market.
