## How Does Airo AI Builder Choose a Design for Your Site?

The process is conversational, not technical.

### Step 1: Describe your business

Tell Airo AI Builder what your business does, who your customers are, and the feeling you want your site to convey. Use plain language: "I run a ceramics studio in Austin, handmade pieces, earthy and organic vibe."

### Step 2: Airo AI Builder selects a matching visual system

Based on your description, it picks a design system that fits. A bold design agency gets vibrant colors and sharp typography. A wellness brand gets soft tones and calming layouts. A healthcare provider gets clean, trustworthy aesthetics.

### Step 3: Your site builds with that design baked in

Every page, every section, every element follows the same visual rules. Headers, buttons, cards, footers: all cohesive, all intentional.

### Step 4: Change the vibe anytime

Not what you were going for? Just describe a different direction. "Make it warmer." "More playful." "I want it to feel premium." Airo AI Builder reskins the entire site to match.
