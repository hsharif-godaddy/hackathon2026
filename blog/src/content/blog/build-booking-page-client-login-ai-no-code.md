## Build a Booking Page in One Prompt

If you run a service business (mechanic, salon, tutor, cleaner, coach, groomer, photographer, trainer), your customers need a way to pick a service, choose an available time, and book it without messaging you back and forth.

Here's how to build that with Airo AI Builder.

### Write the Prompt

Tell Airo AI Builder three things: what you're building, who it's for, and what it needs to do. For a booking page, your prompt should cover three steps in the customer flow:

1. **Pick a service** - List what you offer with approximate duration for each. The calendar uses this to block the right amount of time.
2. **Pick a time** - Specify your business hours and days off. The system shows only available slots.
3. **Confirm** - Collect the customer's name and phone number. Show a confirmation on screen. Send the booking details to your inbox and a confirmation email to the customer.

That's one prompt. The whole booking chain generates from it.

### What You Get

Once Airo AI Builder processes that prompt, you have a live page where:

- Customers see your services with time estimates
- They pick a date from a calendar
- Available slots show up in 15-minute increments based on the service they chose
- They enter their info and confirm
- You both get an email confirmation

It works. You can test it immediately.

### Then Make It Yours

The first version is the working foundation. From there, you shape it with short follow-up prompts:

- Customize the confirmation email (include your business name, the service, and the date in the subject line)
- Add a "first visit" question
- Include directions in the confirmation
- Add a deposit or payment step
- Turn on SMS confirmations

One prompt per change. Each one builds on what's already there.

### Check It on Mobile

Most of your customers will book from their phone. Airo AI Builder generates responsive pages, but always preview the mobile view before you publish. Make sure the flow feels right on a small screen.

## Build a Client Login with a Private Dashboard

If you deliver work to clients (designs, files, reports, tracks, proposals), you know the cycle: you send a link, they lose it, they ask for it again, they open the wrong version. You spend 20 minutes a week just finding the right file for someone.

The fix is a private dashboard. Each client logs in and sees only their own stuff. You upload new versions once. They always know where to look.

This works whether you're a designer, consultant, producer, agency, accountant, coach, or anyone with paying clients who need access to files.

### Write the Prompt

One prompt covers everything. Here's what to include:

**For your clients:**
- Sign-up collects name, email, and password (with a show/hide toggle and strength check)
- After sign-up, they get a verification email, then land on their private dashboard
- Login with email and password, plus a forgot-password link
- Their dashboard shows a personal greeting and a list of files you've shared with them (title, version, notes)
- If you haven't shared anything yet, they see a friendly message instead of a blank page
- The dashboard is protected: if someone tries to access it without logging in, they get sent to the login screen

**For you (the admin):**
- A separate admin account that only you can access
- A list of all your clients with their name, email, sign-up date, and last login
- A form to upload files for a specific client (title, version label, note, file)
- The ability to edit, replace, or delete files
- Clients cannot access the admin area

### What You Get

Airo AI Builder handles the authentication setup automatically. Sign-up, login, password reset, email verification, and route protection all come from that single prompt.

For admin access, it creates a one-time link. You sign up as a regular user first, then visit that link to promote yourself to admin. The link disables itself after one use.

A second prompt builds the full admin panel: your client list as cards, file management with add/edit/delete, uploads, and per-client privacy so nobody sees anyone else's stuff.

### Two Views, One App

**What your client sees:** Their name at the top, a list of what you've shared with them (version numbers, notes, download links).

**What you see:** Every client, their activity, and full control to upload, edit, or remove files per person.
