## Redesigned Editor

We shipped a redesigned develop page (editor) that gives you more room to focus on your actual build.

**What changed:**

- More vertical space for previewing your site while you build
- Improved mobile layout so the editor works better on smaller screens
- A new tab bar for cleaner navigation between code, preview, and settings
- A new banner system that sets us up to deliver more helpful in-app guidance over time

The goal was simple: less friction, more focus.

---

## Built-in Analytics Dashboard

Starter plan users now get a full analytics dashboard inside the app. No extra tools, no third-party integrations.

**What you can track:**

- **Traffic** to your published app
- **Geography** of your visitors
- **Engagement KPIs** like page views and session behavior

We also added **7 new funnel events** so you can analyze drop-off from your homepage all the way through to the agent. This gives you real visibility into how visitors move through your app and where they leave.

Analytics are available after your app is published.

---

## Professional & Ultimate Plans

Professional and Ultimate pricing tiers are live. Both plans give you higher published app limits and larger monthly credit allotments, built for power users who have been leaning on credit packs to keep shipping.

**What's included:**

- Higher published app limits
- Bigger monthly credit allotments
- In-product upgrade and downgrade (change your plan whenever your needs change)
- Backend optimizations to how credits are consumed

If you've been stacking credit packs, these plans give you a cleaner, more predictable way to scale.

---

## Smarter AI Agent

The AI agent now asks clarifying questions before it starts generating your project. With our Claude 4.6 readiness upgrade, we overhauled the agent logic so it confirms key details (like whether your app needs authentication, what pages you want, or what style you're going for) before making assumptions.

Better clarification means fewer rebuilds and more accurate results on the first try.

---

## Faster Builds

Build times dropped from **14 minutes to under 3 minutes**. Same prompt, way less waiting.

We also improved WebSocket logic for instant agent connections, added client-side compression for faster image uploads, and optimized startup times across the board. The whole builder just feels faster.

---

## Custom Domains in Preview

You can now see available custom domains matched to your app's name right inside the preview header. Extensions like .com, .co, .ai, .org, and .store show up automatically. Pick one and go live without leaving the builder.

---

## Contact Form to Email

You can now wire up your contact form to send submissions straight to your inbox. Try this prompt:

> "Have the contact form send me an email at [your business email]"

The builder handles the rest. No extra tools, no Zapier, no code.

## UX Improvements

A few quality-of-life upgrades based on feedback from the community:

- **Git history pagination** so it's easier to navigate changes over time as your project grows
