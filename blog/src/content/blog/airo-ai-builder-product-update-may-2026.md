## Quick Edit: Change Your Site Without Spending Credits

The most requested feature this month: edit your site directly in the preview without using AI credits.

Hover over any element to see a toolbar with Quick Edit options. Change text, adjust colors, and toggle bold or italic styling right where you see it. No prompts, no credits consumed, no waiting for the agent.

For bigger changes, use the Reference button to send that element to chat and let the AI handle complex edits.

---

## Mobile Editing

Quick Edit now works on mobile. A wand button in the chat toolbar toggles inline edit mode. Tap any element to open its toolbar, which repositions automatically to stay visible at screen edges.

No extra clicks required. Tap, edit, done.

## Instant Feedback After Your First Build

Right after Airo AI Builder finishes your initial site, you can give a quick thumbs-up or thumbs-down. This helps us learn which builds delight you and which need work.

Simple feedback, better results over time.

## Getty Images in Search

Image search now includes Getty Images alongside existing sources. When you open the image picker, the search bar auto-populates based on your content and returns relevant Getty results immediately.

Use View More to expand past the default three results. Professional, licensed visuals without leaving the builder.

## Your Site Now Shows Up in Google

Sites built with Airo AI Builder now render on the server, so Google, Bing, and social previews see real content instead of a blank page.

Every published site also gets automatic `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` files. Page titles and descriptions are set in one well-known file the agent can edit reliably.

If your customers find you through search, this is a significant upgrade. For a deeper look at how SEO works in Airo AI Builder, read [Your Website Is Live. But Is It Getting Found?](/blog/get-website-found-on-google-airo-ai-builder-seo).

## Domain Management Gets Its Own Tab

Domain is now a first-class view in the header on desktop, alongside Preview, Edit, Code, and Brand. No more digging through Settings to find it.

On mobile, Domain appears as the second entry in the Settings menu. The domain-attach and transfer flow for external domains is now available for all customers. If you have more than one domain, the agent can also walk you through forwarding one domain to another.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Quick Edit in Airo AI Builder?**
Quick Edit lets you change text and colors directly on your site preview without using AI credits. Hover over any element on desktop or tap on mobile to access the editing toolbar.

**Does Quick Edit use my AI credits?**
No. Quick Edit changes are made directly to your site without going through the AI agent, so no credits are consumed.

**How do I get my Airo AI Builder site to show up on Google?**
Sites built with Airo AI Builder now render on the server automatically. Published sites include a sitemap.xml and robots.txt file so search engines can index your content.

**Can I connect my existing calendar to my Airo AI Builder site?**
Yes. The Bring Your Own Calendar feature supports Google Calendar, Outlook, and iCloud. Your availability syncs in real time and confirmed appointments write back to your calendar automatically.

**What are brand kits in Airo AI Builder?**
Brand kits are pre-designed styling systems that apply cohesive colors, fonts, and visual treatments to your site. Airo AI Builder now includes 12 new brand kits that are auto-selected based on your project.

**How do I share my site before publishing?**
Generate a view-only link from your project. Recipients can preview your site without needing an Airo AI Builder account.

**Can I accept payments on my Airo AI Builder site?**
Yes. Ask the agent to add Stripe payments and it will set up checkout for you. Order IDs display correctly on the success page and customer info pre-fills from your GoDaddy account.
