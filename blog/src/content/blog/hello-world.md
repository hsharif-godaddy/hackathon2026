The Difference Between a Good Prompt and a Great App. Airo AI Builder turns what you type into a working web app. The quality of what you get depends almost entirely on the quality of what you ask for. A vague prompt like "make me a website" forces the AI to guess everything: your industry, your audience, your pages, your features. A specific prompt gives it a clear picture, and you get something usable on the first try. This guide breaks down what makes a prompt work, with real before-and-after examples across the most common use cases.

## Be Specific About Your Business

Instead of saying "I need a website for my business," tell Airo exactly what kind of business you run:

- "I run a mobile dog grooming service in Austin, Texas"
- "I'm a freelance wedding photographer based in Chicago"
- "I own a small bakery that specializes in custom cakes"

The more context you provide, the better Airo can tailor the design, content, and features to your specific needs.

## Describe Your Target Audience

Who are you trying to reach? Let Airo know:

- "My customers are busy professionals aged 25-45"
- "I serve local families looking for weekend activities"
- "My target market is small business owners who need accounting help"

## List the Pages and Features You Need

Don't leave it to chance. Tell Airo exactly what you want:

- "I need a home page, about page, services page, and contact form"
- "Include a gallery to showcase my work and a booking calendar"
- "Add a FAQ section and testimonials from past clients"
