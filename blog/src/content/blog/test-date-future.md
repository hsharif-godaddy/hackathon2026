It's been a busy month at Airo AI Builder. Here's what's new.

## Improved AI Generation

We've significantly upgraded our AI model's understanding of complex prompts. It now handles multi-page sites with interconnected features much better than before. If you've tried building something ambitious and hit limitations, give it another shot.

## New Template Categories

Based on your feedback, we've added starter templates for:

- **Restaurants & Cafes** - Menus, online ordering, reservations
- **Professional Services** - Law firms, consultants, agencies
- **Fitness & Wellness** - Class schedules, membership management
- **Real Estate** - Property listings, agent profiles, contact forms

## Collaboration Features

You can now invite team members to work on your Airo projects:

- Share editing access with colleagues
- Leave comments and suggestions
- Track changes and version history

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where custom fonts weren't loading on mobile
- Improved performance for sites with large image galleries
- Resolved edge cases in the booking calendar component

## What's Coming

We're working on some exciting features for Q2, including enhanced e-commerce capabilities and deeper integrations with third-party tools. Stay tuned!
