## Business-Type Strategies

### Local Restaurant

- Focus on local SEO and "near me" searches
- Highlight unique menu items or dining experience
- Answer questions like "gluten-free options?" or "good for groups?"
- Emphasize what makes them different from other local restaurants
- Use `LocalBusiness` schema with real address and hours

### B2B SaaS

- Target problem-aware searches ("how to reduce customer churn")
- Build thought leadership content
- Optimize for comparison searches vs competitors
- Focus on ROI and business outcomes
- Use `Organization` schema with product/service offerings

### E-commerce Brand

- Optimize for product-specific searches
- Build trust signals (reviews, guarantees)
- Answer buyer questions proactively
- Highlight unique product benefits
- Use `Product` schema per product page

### Personal Portfolio / Freelancer

- Focus on name recognition and skill-specific keywords
- Showcase credentials and project outcomes
- Target "hire [role] in [city]" and service-specific searches
- Use `Person` schema with `sameAs` links to profiles

### Professional Services (Legal, Medical, Finance)

- Emphasize E-E-A-T signals (credentials, certifications)
- Target "near me" + service type searches
- FAQ content addressing common client questions
- Use `ProfessionalService` or specific subtypes

## Modern SEO Considerations

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

- Structure content for AI assistants and featured snippets
- Use clear headings with concise, direct answers below
- Provide factual, well-organized information
- Think about how AI would summarize the page

### User Experience Signals

- Page speed and Core Web Vitals affect rankings
- Mobile-first design is critical (Google indexes mobile)
- Prioritize optimizations based on actual user behavior
- Local businesses should focus especially on mobile UX

### E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)

- Showcase credentials where they matter for the business type
- Build trust signals appropriate to the industry
- Highlight real experience (case studies, testimonials)
- Author attribution for content where expertise matters

## Context-Aware Recommendations

### Bad (Generic)

- "Add an FAQ section"
- "Include more keywords"
- "Add meta descriptions"

### Good (Context-Aware)

- "Add an FAQ addressing common concerns about sustainable sourcing that your eco-conscious customers care about"
- "Incorporate 'farm-to-table Austin' naturally in your homepage hero section to capture local searches"
- "Write meta descriptions that emphasize your 24-hour delivery guarantee — a key differentiator from competitors"

### Principles

Every recommendation should:
1. Reference specific business context (audience, USP, location)
2. Target real search behavior for that niche
3. Explain why it matters for THIS business, not SEO in general
4. Be immediately actionable (specific page, specific change)

Generic advice is noise. The agent has full access to the site content and audit results — use them to make every suggestion specific.
