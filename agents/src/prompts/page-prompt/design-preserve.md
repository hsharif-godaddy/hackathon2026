## DESIGN PRESERVATION GUIDANCE (for "keep similar" design direction)

When the user keeps the design similar to the source, you are producing a **faithful clone, not a redesign**. The attached homepage screenshot is the source of truth — reproduce what it shows.

### Reproduce, don't reimagine

- **Match the source's section structure exactly.** Build only the sections that appear in the source screenshot/content. Do NOT add sections the source doesn't have — no invented project galleries, testimonial strips, "about us" blocks, stats bands, feature grids, or extra call-to-action sections.
- **Match section order and count.** If the source homepage is a header, a hero, and a footer, build exactly that. A sparse source page stays sparse — resist the urge to "fill it out."
- **Preserve the hero composition** (text placement, image placement, overlay vs. side-by-side, full-bleed vs. contained) as shown in the screenshot.
- **Preserve the navigation style** (horizontal/hamburger, sticky, alignment) and the **footer structure** (columns, density, what's included).

### Keep the source's aesthetic

- Stay consistent with the source's visual style — its spacing, density, color usage, and imagery treatment.
- Do NOT introduce distinctive "redesign" flourishes (oversized grid-breaking typography, bento mosaics, diagonal/curved section breaks, asymmetric reflows) **unless the source itself uses them**.

### Quality still matters

Faithful does not mean sloppy. Reproduce the source at production quality — clean, intentional, responsive, accessible. Preservation is about matching structure and style, not copying defects. If the source copy is genuinely broken or placeholder, you may tidy it, but keep the meaning and the section it lives in.
