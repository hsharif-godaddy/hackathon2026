# Chango + Teachers

A playful, approachable, bold, and energetic font pairing.

## Best For

- Children's boutiques
- Toy stores
- Ice cream shops
- Craft stores
- Casual eateries
- Family-friendly businesses
- Youth-oriented brands
- Fun retail shops

## Style Keywords

Playful, fun, energetic, bold, approachable, casual, joyful, youthful, friendly, informal, vibrant, cheerful

## Target Audience

Families with young children, Gen Z consumers, anyone seeking an informal and joyful brand experience.

## Design Pairings

Works well with bright colors, illustrated graphics, hand-drawn elements, casual photography, and short headlines.

## Header Font: Chango

A thick, rounded block font with chunky proportions. Best for short headline copy.

## Body Font: Teachers

A friendly, readable sans-serif for body text.
