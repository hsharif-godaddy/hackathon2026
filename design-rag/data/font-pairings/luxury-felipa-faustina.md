# Felipa + Faustina

An elegant, prestigious font pairing steeped in tradition.

## Best For

- Boutique law firms
- Upscale restaurants
- Artisanal craft studios
- Historic bed & breakfasts
- Bespoke tailoring shops
- Fine dining establishments
- Luxury hotels
- Heritage brands

## Style Keywords

Elegant, prestigious, traditional, heritage, classic, refined, upscale, timeless, sophisticated, Old World, artisanal, bespoke

## Target Audience

Affluent clients seeking premium services, heritage enthusiasts, customers who value timeless quality and Old World craftsmanship over modern trends.

## Design Pairings

Works well with classic color palettes, lightly textured backgrounds (linen, parchment), detail-oriented design elements like borders or crests.

## Header Font: Felipa

A formal calligraphic display typeface with historical roots.

## Body Font: Faustina

An elegant serif with classic proportions.
