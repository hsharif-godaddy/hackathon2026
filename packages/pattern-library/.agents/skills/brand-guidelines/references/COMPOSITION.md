# Composition & Application

> Our finest, brightest and boldest.

This reference covers how all GoDaddy brand elements come together in practice — storytelling, typographic rules, product showcasing, and accessibility requirements.

## Tell a Story

Our photography taps into the life of the everyday entrepreneur while GoDaddy products help them on their journey. Whether it is a presentation, product page or advertisement — be a storyteller.

**Key Principles:**
- Every piece of creative should tell a story about the entrepreneur's journey
- Photography and product UI should work together to create narrative
- The GoDaddy brand is the supporting character; the entrepreneur is the hero

## Type Rules

Our typefaces should not be an afterthought in your projects. Use typographic hierarchy to incite elegance and drama.

**Composition Guidelines:**
- Use GD Sherpa Display for headlines to create visual impact
- Use GD Sherpa Text for supporting information
- Create clear hierarchy between headline, subhead, body, and caption
- Let typography breathe with appropriate whitespace
- Match type weight to the visual weight of accompanying imagery

## Show It Off

GoDaddy products play a vital role in our relationship with entrepreneurs. Whether photography or illustration, GoDaddy products should be prominently and professionally represented.

**Product Presentation Rules:**
- Products should be clearly visible and recognizable
- Device screens should show realistic, polished UI
- Product context should feel natural and authentic
- Avoid cluttered product displays
- Ensure product screenshots are current and accurate

## Digital Contrast Accessibility

### Screen Only

These color combos can be used on screen when using our approved typefaces. They pass standard accessibility requirements starting at:

- **16pt** for GD Sherpa
- **30pt** for GD Sage

**Important:** Combos not listed as approved cannot be used in any digital design with brand typefaces.

### Accessibility Rules

1. **Always check contrast ratios** — Use WCAG 2.1 AA standards as a minimum
2. **Minimum text sizes** — GD Sherpa at 16pt, GD Sage at 30pt for accessible contrast
3. **Test across devices** — Contrast may vary across screens and lighting conditions
4. **Do not rely on color alone** — Use additional visual indicators (icons, underlines) for meaning
5. **Background matters** — Ensure sufficient contrast between text and background colors

## Best Practices

1. **Storytelling first** — Every design tells the entrepreneur's story
2. **Typography creates drama** — Use hierarchy purposefully, not as an afterthought
3. **Products are heroes** — Show them prominently, professionally, and currently
4. **Accessibility is non-negotiable** — Test contrast ratios and meet WCAG AA standards
5. **All elements work together** — Color, type, photography, and illustration should feel unified
6. **Get assets from the official library** — [assets.godaddy](https://assets.godaddy/) is the source of truth
