# Logo & Identity System

> Our logo is our symbol of championship for the everyday entrepreneur we serve.

## Logo and Hierarchy

Our logo is our symbol of championship for the everyday entrepreneur we serve. The GoDaddy identity system has a clear hierarchy of elements that work together to represent the brand.

## Lockup

Our lockup is how we announce our brand to the world.

**Clear Space Rule:** Our lockup needs to breathe. Clear space protects its integrity and clarity. Use the width of the "o" in GoDaddy to keep intruding elements at bay.

### Lockup Rules

- Always maintain the required clear space around the lockup
- Never modify, distort, or recreate the lockup
- Use the lockup on contrasting backgrounds for maximum impact

## The GO

The GO is a clear statement of advocacy for entrepreneurs everywhere — a symbol of empowerment that encourages them to stand on their own two feet.

**Clear Space Rule:** To present the GO in the most favorable light, use the width of the "o" in the GoDaddy wordmark as your guide for clear space.

## Registration and Trademarks

Our primary lockup and GO icon no longer feature the registered trademark symbol (R). All compliance and information specific to trademark can be found on the legal page of [GoDaddy.com](http://godaddy.com/).

## Placement Rules

Our lockup has maximum impact when it appears on a contrasting color — ideally Cash Black, Space White, or Everyday Teal.

**Key Rules:**
- Place the logo on contrasting backgrounds for readability
- If the logo must be placed on an image, it should be the focal point and fully readable
- Preferred backgrounds: Cash Black (`#111111`), Space White (`#FFFFFF`), Everyday Teal 400 (`#1BDBDB`)

## GoDaddy Registry

GoDaddy Registry is the independent domain name registry arm of GoDaddy. It is a sub-brand of GoDaddy, with its unique visual design language and writing tenets. See the [GoDaddy Registry Brand Guidelines](https://www.assets.godaddy/l/5b765a72ded39895/) for details.

## Business Units — Internal Use Only

Each GoDaddy business unit has a specialty internal use only logo. These marks can be used for:

- Presentations
- Internal communications
- Internal promotion of the business units

For a full overview of the brand structure, see the [GoDaddy Portfolio Guidelines](https://styleguides.godaddy.design/portfolio).

## Acquired Brands

Acquired brands expand our business and product offerings. All acquired brands fit into our brand portfolio structure.

**Rules:**
- Acquired brand lockups are created by the GoDaddy Brand Team and should never be created by individuals
- The GoDaddy wordmark is featured with acquired brand lockups to assist with scale and readability
- The full GoDaddy lockup can be utilized in large scale use cases

## Partnerships and Programs

We lend our primary brand and sub-brand lockups to various scenarios including partnerships, external programs, and technology initiatives.

**Important:** All partnership lockups and program lockups require approval from the GoDaddy Brand Team. Share via `#brand_help` Slack channel for approval.

### Partnership Lockup

GoDaddy has a robust playbook outlining approved structures for brand marketing partnerships, including co-marketing and co-branding structures.

### External Program Lockup

Use when GoDaddy owns the external program or provides key support for the program. Some programs have pre-made logos.

### "Powered by GoDaddy"

The use of "powered by" is for GoDaddy Registry use cases only. It is not approved for broad usage in other naming applications.

## GoDaddy Product Names

GoDaddy product names almost never appear with any additional symbols or wordmarks — no logo or wordmark of GoDaddy's and no special design element of any sort. Following this rule helps keep our brand integrity intact.

**Rules:**
- Product names should be in GD Sherpa Display or GD Sherpa Text
- The word "GoDaddy" should NOT appear in front of the product name
- A handful of products have exception use case lockups (see [Brand Portfolio Guidelines](https://www.assets.godaddy/guidelines/guide/4fb1f6f7-c28a-4260-897c-d9f0f1a624c9/page/abc6ce0c-6839-41ef-b34a-7f69411aaf4a))

## GoDaddy Team Names and Events

GoDaddy team names and events never appear with any additional symbols or wordmarks — no logo, no special design elements.

**Rules:**
- The word "GoDaddy" should NOT appear in front of the team name in type
- Internal team names should strictly use **GD Sherpa Text Medium**

## Trademark Design Violations

Our trademarks were created with specific intent. Please keep their integrity intact.

**Never do the following:**
- Stretch, skew, or distort the logo
- Change the logo colors outside of approved variations
- Add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines) to the logo
- Recreate or modify the logo in any way
- Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
- Crop or partially hide the logo

## Best Practices

1. **Respect the lockup** — Always use official, approved lockup files
2. **Maintain clear space** — Use the "o" width as the minimum clear space
3. **High contrast placement** — Logo on Cash Black, Space White, or Everyday Teal
4. **Get approval** — Partnership and program lockups require Brand Team approval via `#brand_help`
5. **Product names stay clean** — No logos, symbols, or "GoDaddy" prefix with product names
6. **Team names use GD Sherpa Text Medium** — Consistent typography for internal naming
