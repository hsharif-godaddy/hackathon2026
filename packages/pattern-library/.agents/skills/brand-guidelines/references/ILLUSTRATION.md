# Illustration

> We use illustration to illuminate and bring simplicity to complex concepts and simplify product understanding.

## Hand-Drawn Illustration

Hand-drawn illustrations work at a variety of scales:

- **Smaller, single concept illustrations** — Use to support key messages
- **Larger, editorial style illustrations** — Use to paint a full picture of a story

The hand-drawn illustration style is a signature element of the GoDaddy brand, bringing warmth and personality to communications.

## Illustration Library

The full hand-drawn illustration library is available to use and contains a variety of illustrations that focus on big concept ideas all the way to product-specific interpretations.

**Need a custom illustration?** Work with the GoDaddy Brand Team to create new custom illustrations. Do not create illustrations independently.

## Diversity in Hand-Drawn Illustration

An important brand goal is to always highlight diversity, even in illustration. There are numerous ways to accomplish this:

- **Illustrate specific features** of individuals to show diversity and individuality
- **Add skin tone color** to illustrate diversity
- Ensure a range of body types, hair styles, and cultural representations

## Hand-Drawn Illustration Mistakes to Avoid

Hand-drawn illustrations are created to evoke a specific mood and emotion. Avoid these mistakes so they retain maximum impact:

- Do not alter the illustration style or line weight
- Do not recolor illustrations outside of approved palettes
- Do not stretch, skew, or distort illustrations
- Do not combine illustration styles from different sources
- Do not create new illustrations without Brand Team involvement
- Do not use illustrations at sizes that compromise their detail or readability

## Best Practices

1. **Simplify complex concepts** — Illustrations illuminate and bring simplicity
2. **Scale appropriately** — Small for supporting messages, large for storytelling
3. **Use the official library** — All hand-drawn illustrations are available in the asset library
4. **Custom work needs Brand Team** — Never create new illustrations independently
5. **Diversity is required** — Always represent diverse people in illustrations
6. **Maintain the style** — Do not alter line weight, color, or proportions
7. **Context matters** — Match illustration scale and complexity to the communication need
