## Voice Pillars

GoDaddy's voice is how we consistently show up across every channel, product, and touchpoint. It reflects our brand personality — the Doubt Slayer — and shapes how we communicate. While our voice stays constant, our tone flexes to meet the moment, adjusting based on context, audience, and surface.

Our brand voice is made up of three interconnected pillars:

### Forward-Thinking

**Characteristics:** Visionary, Experienced, Approachable

**Meaning:** GoDaddy understands both the dream and the journey, having spent decades anticipating obstacles, creating opportunities, and innovating to meet customers' needs.

**Sounds Like:** Our voice balances future vision with present practicality — we speak with the confidence of experience while maintaining the warmth of a trusted partner, showing we understand both the dream and the journey of small business owners.

**In Product, Sounds Like:** Guidance that knows what's ahead.

### Energizing

**Characteristics:** Catalyzing, Passionate, Optimistic

**Meaning:** GoDaddy bolsters determination through grounded positivity and energetic, action-driving encouragement — all delivered with the unvarnished perspective of a straight-talking ally.

**Sounds Like:** Our action-oriented voice is charged with momentum — using confident declarations and forward-driving language that propels customers from hesitation to action, while weaving in well-timed wit that cuts through tension and makes challenges feel more conquerable.

**In Product, Sounds Like:** Motivation + confidence to take the next step.

### Demystifying

**Characteristics:** Empathetic, Simplifying, Insightful

**Meaning:** GoDaddy stands beside our customers, turning intimidating challenges into clear growth pathways. With a genuine understanding of customer anxieties, we strip away technical jargon and simplify complex digital experiences.

**Sounds Like:** Our voice brings a breath of fresh air to technical topics, using everyday language and clever, relatable metaphors to make technical subjects understandable and engaging.

**In Product, Sounds Like:** Everyday language to clarify technical subjects.

## Voice Flex

At times, you may lean more heavily on one voice pillar to match the creative objective. That is expected, and strategic. These moments of voice flex help drive clarity and impact while still holding true to all three voice pillars.

### Forward-Thinking (Flex Lead)

When the forward-thinking voice is flexed, it is more about the big picture — guiding customers toward what's next and helping them envision possibility.

**Campaign/Awareness Examples:**
- "With Airo, consider your business future proofed."
- "Turn 'Maybe someday' into 'Now Open.'"

**In-Product Examples:**
- "Make sure you save this report. It'll come in handy when your business is up and running!"
- "Claiming the .nets and the .cos will secure your brand as it grows."

### Energizing (Flex Lead)

When the energizing voice is flexed, it is about momentum — rallying customers with enthusiasm, confidence, and a sense of shared excitement.

**Campaign/Awareness Examples:**
- "Give your business some supercharged swagger."
- "With Airo, you got this. And that. And all the other things."
- "What if you could finally do it all? With Airo, you can."

**In-Product Examples:**
- "Let's work on improving your business viability report. I've got some easy suggestions."

### Demystifying (Flex Lead)

When the demystifying voice is flexed, it is about clarity — breaking things down, offering reassurance, and making complex ideas feel simple and doable.

**Campaign/Awareness Examples:**
- "Your ride or die AI that handles the hard stuff."
- "It's not rocket science, unless that's your small business."

**In-Product Examples:**
- "Your site's privacy policy helps protect you from legal liability."
- "Tell me a bit about what your site needs, and I'll help you choose the right builder."

## Tone Spectrum

While our voice pillars are constant, tone helps us meet the moment, shifting based on message type, audience mindset, and surface. Each pillar has tonal range baked into its characteristics. Use this spectrum to guide how you apply tone to voice while keeping GoDaddy's personality intact.

## Voice & Tone Across the Funnel

Our voice sounds different across the brand funnel — from awareness to action — while staying true to our core personality. Every example holds true to our three-pillar personality but adapts in strength and emotion to meet the user where they are.

## Creative Expression

While voice and tone shape our written expression, they can also inform creative direction. Whether it's bold typography, approachable illustration, or confident color combos, our brand's Doubt Slayer personality should influence the tone of all creative work. Start with the voice pillars as inspiration and let them guide the energy, mood, and visual weight of the work, even when words are not present.

## Best Practices

1. **All three pillars are always present** — Even when one takes the lead, the others support in spirit
2. **Tone flexes, voice stays constant** — Adjust tone for context but maintain the core voice
3. **The Doubt Slayer is the personality** — Every communication should feel like it comes from someone who turns complexity into confidence
4. **Be a straight-talking ally** — Grounded positivity, not empty enthusiasm
5. **Strip away jargon** — Use everyday language, especially in product
6. **Action over aspiration** — Drive customers from hesitation to action
7. **Match the funnel stage** — Awareness is bolder; product is clearer and more supportive
8. **Creative direction follows voice** — Let the voice pillars guide visual design choices too
