# Photography

> Our photography philosophy is simple — capture genuine people doing their thing in a real environment. Think bright, bold and natural.

GoDaddy's photography celebrates all the steps of the entrepreneur's emotional journey. We unabashedly love everyday entrepreneurs, and that shows in how we photograph them, with their personality, independence and energy on full display — regardless of whether they are captured in mid-creation or in a spontaneous respite from their work.

## Campaign

Campaign photography evokes positive, my-time-is-now emotion and ignites a get-off-the-couch energy. Its look is purposefully distinct from brand photography because it embodies an aesthetic unique to the campaign.

**Rule:** Never use campaign photography for non-campaign purposes.

**Channels:** Advertising | Out of Home | Front of Site | Social | Email

## Makers

Makers photography inspires future everyday entrepreneurs by showcasing customers who are already making their own way with our help and tools. We make them the heroes of their own stories with art directed photos (mainly through props and lighting) that capture their spirit and style.

**Rule:** Makers require proper attribution.

**Channels:** Advertising OA/OLV | Out of Home | Front of Site | Social | Email

## Icons

Icons have long been our heroes at GoDaddy — they are tireless, creative, diverse, independent. When we photograph Icons, we are capturing real people doing beautiful and interesting things.

**Rule:** Each Icons photo requires attribution.

**Channels:** Social | Email | Blog | PR

## GoDaddy Guides

GoDaddy Guides photography emphasizes approachability and kindness with calm, neutral backgrounds free of distractions. The friendly and welcoming expressions of our Guides serve as the focal point, embodying our commitment to customer support.

**Attribution:** Photography must be attributed. Place attribution to the right of the Guide, close to their head.

**Background Colors:** As GoDaddy Guides has been transitioned to a Premium Product, the background colors in these photos have been updated to align with the GoDaddy Brand, featuring Everyday Teal and Tech Gray.

**Channels:** Front of Site | Social | Email | Blog | PR

### GoDaddy Guides as a Team of Experts

Portrait photos of Guides can be arranged in a lineup to create a dynamic and seamless flow, presenting the team as a welcoming, ever-present support network.

**Rules:**
- Use the lineup structure to complement surfaces referencing Guides' services
- No Guide's head or torso should be cropped or cut off
- Position the Guides close enough to convey unity and cohesion
- Maintain enough spacing for a polished and professional look

## GoDaddy Registry

GoDaddy Registry photography lives at the intersection of humanity, technology and vision. These photos communicate the possibilities that domain extensions make available to the world.

**Channels:** All channels (Advertising OA/OLV, OOH, Front of Site, Social, Email, Blog, PR)

## Products

Device photography shows off our products in the everyday entrepreneur's world. They are art directed photos that brilliantly display the tools we offer to help folks bring their goals to life.

**Channels:** All channels

## Photography Attributions

In the interest of consistency and simplicity, a standard attribution format exists across Makers, Icons and Guides. Only the types of information shown will differ, depending on the program.

**Rule:** Utilize only **GD Sherpa Text Regular** for attributions.

## Photography Mistakes to Avoid

It is vitally important to know which photography traps to avoid. What might seem like a good idea could drastically impact how the brand is portrayed, compromising the aesthetic we work hard to create.

**Avoid:**
- Overly posed or staged-looking shots
- Poor lighting or low-quality images
- Cluttered or distracting backgrounds
- Images that feel generic or stock-like
- Lack of diversity in subjects
- Improper or missing attribution
- Using campaign photography outside campaigns

## GoDaddy Stock

GoDaddy stock is a collection of images created by the GoDaddy team that can be used across all creative and does not require attribution. Images are available on [assets.godaddy](https://www.assets.godaddy/media/?resetsearch=&field=metaproperty_AssetType%2Cmetaproperty_Stock&value=Photography%2CGDStock).

**Channels:** All channels

## Using Stock Photography

Stock photography should be used sparingly. Where necessary, use assets from [assets.godaddy](https://assets.godaddy/) or the GoDaddy Stock Library to best align with brand customer guidelines.

**External Stock Rules:**
- **Getty is the only brand-approved external stock photography resource**
- While assets.godaddy and GoDaddy Stock assets are free to use, Getty Images are not free
- Teams should plan to cover costs and any post-production needed for brand alignment
- Only purchase **royalty-free** assets from Getty

## Stock Photography Mistakes to Avoid

Stock photography comes with its own set of rules. Common mistakes to avoid:

- Images that look obviously staged or stock-like
- Generic business imagery (handshakes, suits, glass buildings)
- Images that do not align with GoDaddy's authentic, entrepreneurial aesthetic
- Using non-approved stock sources (only Getty is approved)
- Not post-processing to align with brand standards

## Best Practices

1. **Authenticity first** — Capture genuine people in real environments
2. **Bright, bold, natural** — The GoDaddy photography philosophy
3. **Respect category boundaries** — Campaign photos stay in campaigns
4. **Always attribute** — Makers, Icons, and Guides require attribution
5. **GD Sherpa Text Regular for attributions** — Consistent typography
6. **Getty only for external stock** — The only approved external source, royalty-free only
7. **Diversity matters** — Showcase diverse entrepreneurs and experiences
8. **Products in context** — Show GoDaddy products in the entrepreneur's real world
