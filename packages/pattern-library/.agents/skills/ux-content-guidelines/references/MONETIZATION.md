# Monetization Content Strategy

> UX Content Design strategy for monetization, upgrades, and customer growth messaging.

## Background

With Airo, we have the opportunity to offer an innovative, net-new experience, but how we're currently doing that can be confusing and inconsistent. We aim to bring much more holistic strategy to how we grow customers' ventures and hit company goals through UX and messaging strategy.

## Strategy Foundations

| Foundation | Guiding Question |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Company Mission** | How might we empower entrepreneurs everywhere and make opportunity more inclusive for all? |
| **Global Value Prop** | How might we accelerate growth for unstoppable confidence? |
| **Brand Persona** | How might we embody the Doubt Slayer in how we guide customers? |
| **UX Pillar** | How might we create experiences that are effective, efficient, and enjoyable? |
| **Product Value Prop** | How might we message the value of individual products? |

## Customer Point of View

- **Big ideas, limited knowledge:** Entrepreneurs come to us with big ideas but maybe not a lot of knowledge on the steps they need to take.
- **Too much info is overwhelming:** Choice paralysis when offered too many options without context related to their journey.
- **Not enough info creates roadblocks:** Surprise steep costs or high difficulty erode trust.
- **We need to offer a clear path:** Build guideposts along the way to build confidence and allow focus on the path ahead.

## Key UX Messaging Strategies

### 1. Energize: Build Momentum

How might we energize customers? By identifying and seizing upon the right moments for building momentum.

- Identify specific high-value features most relevant to the user at their stage.
- Focus the message on completing the action that's top of mind.

**Pattern — Rightsizing Freemium:** Copy focuses on what you *can do*, not everything you can get.

**Pattern — Premium Tab in WAM:** Copy focuses on a single benefit in a moment when users are setting up their site.

### 2. Support: Initiate Two-Way Conversations

How do we support customers? By asking questions that imply value and build confidence.

- Lead with questions that imply value.
- Be aware of how often in the UX we're telling the user to do something.

**Question Guidelines:**
- The question needs to be supported by the overall UX.
- Does the CTA work when there's a question in H1?
- Ensure there's value implied — open-ended questions aren't part of this strategy.
- Is it an easy "yes!" or "no" answer? This is a handy way to gut-check yourself.

### 3. Forward-Thinking: Bite-Sized Next Steps

How do we offer forward-thinking solutions? By giving customers bite-sized next steps rather than overwhelming them.

- Position upgrades as bite-sized next steps.
- Be clear and specific.

**Pattern — Editor Paywall:** Copy equates full site access as a logical next step.

**Pattern — Value Achievers Upgrade:** Copy acknowledges a user win and focuses on upgrade as the next step.

## What Doesn't Work

### Don't Overuse "Free"

- Customers are sensitive to price info and can easily feel misled if something is "free" for a limited time.
- When we save vital information for a checkout flow, we risk eroding trust.
- "Free" can sometimes make a tool seem less valuable, not more.

### Avoid Over-Explaining

- Customers can only absorb so much. Explaining what a feature is, how it works, why they want it, AND that it's free is too much.
- Stick to carrots that are immediately understandable and valuable.

### Be Clear on Value

- If we aren't clear on the value we're offering and how it fits the user's goals, we won't convince them.
- The "why" needs to be clear.

## Strategy Recap

1. Identify and seize the right moments for building momentum.
2. Focus on high-value features that are top of mind for the customer.
3. Ask questions that imply value — try not to tell users to do too much.
4. Position upgrades as bite-sized next steps.
5. Don't overuse "free."
6. Be specific and explain the why.

## Future Directions

- Test upgrade messaging that leads with loss aversion.
- Explore personalized messaging letting users know one key thing they'll gain from upgrading.
- Explore the effectiveness of "no"-oriented questions.
