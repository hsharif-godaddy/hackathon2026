# Writing for Mobile

> Mobile-specific content rules and checklist for GoDaddy product experiences.

## Mobile Content Checklist

In addition to the standard Content Checklist, here are 4 things to remember when writing content for mobile without a Content Designer.

### 1. Keep It Short

We should always avoid overwriting, but it's crucial on mobile where space is tight. Keep it simple and only include what's necessary to get the message across.

### 2. Avoid Big Chunks of Copy

If you have a big block of copy after cutting redundancy, make it more scannable with bullets or line breaks. Keep the most important info at the top.

### 3. Prioritize the Info Customers Need Right Now

For info that's nice to know but isn't crucial for the customer to move forward, include it in a link or a tooltip.

### 4. Make Space for Translation

Your copy will grow by about 30% when translated. To see how it'll fit, translate it into a language with longer words, like German.

## Mobile Style Rules

### Em Dashes on Mobile

In mobile surfaces — to save space — cut the spaces on either side of an em dash.

> Desktop: Our GoDaddy Guides — available 24/7 — are waiting for your call.
>
> Mobile: Our GoDaddy Guides—available 24/7—are waiting for your call.

### Click vs Tap

When writing for mobile, say "tap," not "click."

### Bold for CTAs

For mobile, use bold when guiding the customer to a CTA in copy.

### Snackbars

Keep copy short — no more than 2 lines of text. Use complete sentences with closing punctuation. CTAs should be in title case, not all caps.

### Section Titles and Headlines

Follow the same formatting rules as desktop: section titles in title case (no punctuation), headlines in sentence case (no period for single sentences).
