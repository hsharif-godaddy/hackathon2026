# Formatting Rules

> Numbers, dates, times, pricing, abbreviations, and other formatting standards for GoDaddy product content.

## Abbreviations and Acronyms

You may know what it means, but does the reader? If you're not 100% sure, spell it out (at least in the first usage on the page). Well-known exceptions like HTML or CSS can stay abbreviated.

## All Caps

Avoid ALL CAPS. We don't need to shout to get our point across. The only exception is tags (e.g., "NEW," "FREE").

## Bolding

Since we avoid italics and quotation marks, use bolding to add emphasis. Bold domain names, parts of the product, and statuses.

- Congrats! You've secured **hikingboots.com**
- Go to your **Dashboard** to complete this action.
- Success! Your order has been marked as **Fulfilled**.

## Numbers

Always use numerals, even at the beginning of a sentence. It's easier for readers to scan.

> **Correct:** 4 out of 10 customers like you chose this plan.
>
> **Incorrect:** Four out of 10 customers like you chose this plan.

For numbers in the millions, write it out:

> **Correct:** Two million entrepreneurs in the US use Microsoft email products.
>
> **Incorrect:** 2,000,000 entrepreneurs...

## Dates

When space provides, spell out the day and month:

> Tuesday, October 11, 2016

If you must abbreviate, shorten the month (no period on the abbreviation):

> Oct 11, 2016

- Try to avoid using only digits to prevent confusion between US and international formatting.
- International dates are written: Year, Month, Day (2016 October 11).

> **Incorrect:** 11/11/2016 / Oct. 11, 2016

## Times and Time Zones

- Use lowercase am and pm without a space before them: 7 pm, 10 am
- Use uppercase for time zones: EST, CST, MST, PST
- Combined: 7 pm MST
- Use an en-dash without spaces for ranges: Mon–Fri 8 am–3 pm MST

### Abbreviating Hours and Minutes

When possible, don't abbreviate "hours" and "minutes":

> **Correct:** This could take up to 30 minutes.

In spatial constraints (like drop-down lists), use "hrs" and "min":
- Every 30 min
- Every 1 hr
- Every 2 hrs

For contexts where seconds matter, use single characters:

> Your last backup: 1h 12m 23s

## Pricing, Money, and Currency

- No cents for full dollar amounts. Use abbreviations for term of price.
- For cents, simplify to the cent symbol: 99¢ (instead of $0.99)

> **Correct:** $199/yr / 99¢

## Term Lengths

Abbreviate, no period:

> **Correct:** $1/mo or $12/yr

Note: "yr" must appear next to a number (e.g., "$12/yr") and should not be used for general terms (e.g., "Secure your discount with a multi-year term").

## Domain Names

Domains are valuable assets. Talk about them in ways that reassure users we handle them with care.

### Domain Actions Defined

| Action | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| Register | Reserving a domain name on the Internet for a set period of time |
| Connect | Linking a domain name to a URL |
| Transfer | Changing the organization that registers a domain |
| Forward | Redirecting a domain to point to a site external to GoDaddy |
| Park | Connecting a domain to a temporary "parked page" provided by GoDaddy |
| Update destination | Changing the URL that the domain is connected to |

### Domain States

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Connected | Domain is connected to a site |
| Not connected | Domain is not connected to a site |
| Pending | Domain is in the process of being connected |

### Domain Names In-Product

- Be mindful that long domains might cause a line break.
- Put domain names in the beginning or middle of a sentence to avoid adding punctuation to the URL, or use an eyebrow instead of a header.
- Stick to lowercase unless you know the domain is capitalized in a specific way (GoDaddy.com).

## Email, ecommerce, and ebook

All are one word, lowercase (unless beginning a sentence). No hyphen.

> **Correct:** Email is a vital way of reaching your customers.
>
> **Incorrect:** Would you like to send your customers an Email?

## Cities and States

Spell out city names and use postal codes for states.

> **Correct:** Scottsdale, AZ
>
> **Incorrect:** Scottsdale, Arizona

## Telephone Numbers

Standard US formatting: (480) 505-8877

- For extra clarity, add +1 before: +1 (480) 505-8877
- International varies by country: +040 4918 7600 (when in doubt, look it up)

## Temperature

Use the degree symbol — F for Fahrenheit and C for Celsius:

> 62˚F / 17˚C

## Formal Titles

Only capitalize titles when they precede someone's name in a sentence.

> **Correct:** Shahzad, our GoDaddy newsletter managing editor, is well known for his recipes.
>
> **Incorrect:** Shahzad, our GoDaddy newsletter Managing Editor, is well known for his recipes.

## URLs in Headlines

While inserting a domain name is great for personalization, be careful — longer domains could cause a line break. Consider adding the personalized URL in accompanying imagery or body copy instead.

## Website and Web Page

- "Website" is one word, not capitalized in a sentence.
- "Web page" is two words, also not capitalized.

> Check out your new website. / Here's a web page.
